We opened in the middle of the fight on the pens ledge, with two owlbears still standing. Garran and Lucien were still reeling from the big one's screech. Ralo went at the small one alone with whip and scimitar and left it hurting. Milo had been blown off the dock by a thunderwave and was hanging off the edge by his hands. He pulled himself up without dropping his Slow. Then he hit the small owlbear with dissonant whispers, and it turned to run for the cave. It ran right past Ralo, and Ralo cut it down on the way by.

The big one only had one attack left in it, and it used it to put Ralo at one hit point. It also shook off the Slow. Ralo crit it back and left it swinging at disadvantage. Milo, Garran and Astrid chipped at it while Pocket flew in to help. Lucien was not sure the crew could survive another screech. His magic surged, and his next damaging spell would hit as hard as it possibly could. His fire bolt did twenty, and the owlbear went up in a cloud of burning feathers.

That made two ledges of the quarry theirs, and no way off them, since the skiff was wrecked on the first one. Olivier spent his last two cure wounds on Ralo and Milo. Kalish has no healing. He is more of a lockpick. Astrid patched up Ralo and herself and held on to the rest of her slots.

Pocket flew into the cave off the bottom ledge and found tunnels that run inside the cliff alongside the ledges. A chute led up to the pens, and further in it forked toward the distillery and the inner sanctum. Pocket took the distillery fork and perched on an iron rung under the floor. Two groups of druids were arguing over who was in charge. One side had orders to get the Widow's Bane out at all costs, and had no use for Mother Lycricia. The other said she had not given the order to fall back, and these people might still be reasoned with. Pocket is a nightjar, and he caught the cheep of a chick. Astrid wanted to set the whole place on fire. She brought him home instead.

They climbed up to the pens. Three hens were chained there in heavy vests that hold their wings shut. The fourth pen held a male on his last legs, his plumage drained of color. He was still sitting on his nest, and three chicks were drinking the last of his tears. The fifth pen was empty. Astrid knew his mate had been taken away so she would not be there when he died, or when they took his chicks. She figured he had a few hours left. Nobody saw anything they could do for him yet, so they took a short rest. Ralo turned up three power crystals for skiffs, two potions of healing, and a spell scroll.

Near the end of the rest, something came down over the quarry wall. It looked like a celestial warthog with pegasus wings and two gnarled tusks, one of them broken off. It landed and asked in Common whether it had to be blood. Astrid said it had been poaching. That caught it off guard, and it turned back into a half-orc woman with a staff.

"Poaching is when you kill the animals." Mother Lycricia called the transformation nature's immune system against a heinous capitalist act of husbandry. Turn a widow or two, and the farming would have to stop. Astrid said the cranes belong in their sanctuary, and are quite happy there. She talks to them. She asked whether Lycricia had.

Lucien read her best. She was profiting off all of it, and her partners had just double-crossed her. She was shopping for a new side. She asked the other four what scoundrels charge. Ralo said nothing would sway him short of an executive producer credit. He is in showbiz now.

So it was blood. The Circle had lost enough today, she said, and would fall back to show it meant them no harm. The Irisours and the Tabula agents would fight to the death, and that was no longer her business. Astrid knew they had the hen, and wanted her back unharmed, in her current form. Lycricia said the Tabula had her in the sanctum for a terrible ritual. The chicks were at the distillery. She hated to make them choose. Astrid told her she would not need to.

Astrid gave her best crane call, and Lycricia sighed and flew back up the cliffs. The call echoed once, then twice, and Livid answered. So did every crane in that quarry and the next one over, and they all went up into the evening sky together. In the Widow, the star at the tip of the beak shone a little brighter.

Kalish turned up at Astrid's elbow. When the druids took him, he had planted a crystal on one of them, and it had been listening the whole time. He wanted it back. They might have said things they would never record on purpose. The chicks at the distillery were Livid's, so Kalish, Olivier and Livid went for the distillery, and the crew went for the sanctum. Astrid was fairly sure Livid would remember they were on her side.

Garran and Ralo took the lead up the switchbacks, which were harder work than the adrenaline had suggested. They both rolled a 19 on stealth, because Garran was carrying Ralo under his arm. At the top, Irisour halflings and Tabula agents had stacked crates into a rough barricade. Behind it was a sunken pit, open to the sky. A hen was chained in the middle of it, still a crane and not yet turned, while their spellcasters worked some macabre ritual on her.

Garran slipped through the crates, dropped into the pit, and called up the wild around him. The azer in front of him was frightened of him for the rest of the fight. Ralo killed an aarakocra on his way in. Milo dropped a Hypnotic Pattern on the crowd, and four of the six it caught froze. An aeromancer tried to counterspell Lucien's Slow. Lucien beat it, and the Slow took four more. Astrid put a guiding bolt into the aeromancer for twenty-two. Then it threw a lightning bolt through Milo, Astrid and Lucien. Lucien hit the ground, and the Slow went with him. The Pattern held.

Ralo spent his action surge to finish the azer, which had been burning Garran every time it ended a turn next to him. Milo got Lucien back up with a healing word. Then the geomancer brought a hail of stone down on the middle of the pit. It put Ralo on the ground and killed one of its own aarakocra. Astrid answered with wither and bloom, which killed another aarakocra and brought Ralo around. An aeromancer flew in on Milo, and Lucien stabbed it on the way past with a jade letter opener. It knocked Milo out anyway, then blew Lucien down the stairs with a gust of wind.

Ralo used his second wind to slip out from between two enemies without drawing a single attack. He spent the movement getting to Milo and poured a potion of healing down his throat. Lucien climbed back up the stairs, read the spell scroll as a fireball, and burned five of them in purple fire. Meanwhile the hen kept her head. Whatever they had been doing to her, she fought it off three times, and she is in no danger of turning now.

Garran put down the lizardfolk sovereign in two hits. Ralo had gone down again at the bottom of the pit, and rolled a natural 20 on his death save. He got up with one hit point, killed the geomancer and an aarakocra, and put a hand crossbow bolt into one more for good measure. Milo rolled a natural 20 on his own death save. "I still haven't tried a blood orange. I have unfinished business on this plane." Lucien popped his head back over the ledge and fire bolted the last aarakocra still fighting, then ducked back down. The only one left was too frightened of Garran to hit him, and Astrid shot it dead.

Then the pit was quiet. The hen was still chained in the middle of it, but she was still herself. Nobody knew yet how Livid, Olivier and Kalish had done at the distillery. We leave our scoundrels there, at the end of a very long day.
