We opened the next morning at the rookery, where Oskra had put the crew up after they saved her people and her cranes. Livid was back with her chicks, and the second hen was free and still herself. Everything had tidied up quite nicely, apart from several of them nearly dying. Oskra paid them in runes. Garran took the armor rune, and Ralo took the weapon rune. Livid cried onto Astrid's boots, and the boots grew wings. The camp also gave up two spell scrolls.

Kalish came by with Olivier. Livid's distraction had let him get his crystal back off the cultist. It records only sound, and it had caught everything the druids, the Irisours and the Tabula said. But the fight had jostled the crystal and corrupted the recording. Astrid asked whether he had tried turning it off and on again. He had. The runes were damaged, and it needed a master gem cutter. Oskra is a card-carrying union woman, and she knew one in the guild. Ralo, as executive producer, was already pitching to kill off Olivier's character and bring in someone new.

They came down off the cliffs into Gemheart, one of Rifthaven's oldest districts and home to its masterwork guilds. Kendrin's shop had stood for generations, with fine work in the windows, and it should have been busy. A poster on the pole outside said to vote Jelfi, and underneath, that Kendrin would abandon you like he had abandoned the race. A worried gnome woman was sweeping the step. She saw the poster, shook her head, and ripped it in half.

Milo said hello. She took in his clothes and invited him in, since the shop had not had a patron in a while. Then she saw the armor on Ralo and Garran and offered them resurrection diamonds. Milo told her his friends were all alive, and these were all the friends he had in the world. Astrid asked why everyone took them for adventurers. Two of them were in studded leather, the gnome said, and they carried more weapons than the city guard. Her name was Facette, after her father's favorite things. Her sister got Clarity.

Facette had just made journeyman, and the real master was her father. He had gone missing during Glittergold's Holy Week, with the guild election closing tonight. He had won six in a row. Then her rival Jelfi got far too good, far too fast, without the proper training. Now the heads of the bankers, the bakers and the basket weavers were wearing Jelfi's rings and calling themselves uncertain. Facette had been whispering into the night for help, and here they were. She asked them to prove it, or to do something so nobody had to, and wondered whether she had said that out loud. The crew needed a master gem cutter either way. Lucien was interested in the mind control jewelry. They went to the Guild Hall.

Inside, the hall was caucusing around a few important people. A halfling bricklayer spotted Lucien and was thrilled to see Mr. Wolf down here in person. Lucien had never seen him before. The man had him confused with Lucious Wolf, the divorce attorney who advertises in the drunk tank. He still had the flyer, which showed a heavy man with greasy slicked-back hair. The boys spend a night in the tank, their ladies refuse to post bail, and the boys get ideas. Somewhere in the city, a doppelganger has been using Lucien's likeness. It explains the ad Ralo saw in the urinal at Bennigan's.

Lucien turned down the bricklayer's introductions and had him point people out from a distance. Olo, the dwarf, was the incumbent for the Bankers Guild and took bribes. Lucien asked whether bribe-taking bankers were usually nice people. In the bricklayer's experience, they always said thank you. Soraya, the old-money elf, ran the Basket Weavers, and they voted as a block. Briska, a tiefling shaping iron with her bare hands, was favored for the Farriers. Lucien looked at their hands. Olo wore a gaudy net of caged gems in his beard. Briska had none, not even in her horns. Soraya had the dent of a ring on her finger, and no ring.

Lucien cast Detect Thoughts and went to see Briska. She told him he was awfully pretty, and she had taken a hundred meetings with his type this week. In her head, his ascot was too nice for the district, and he had used five hedge words in a ten-word sentence. When he brought up her lack of jewelry, her guard went up. Plenty of her colleagues had suddenly taken to jewelry, she said, but her hands were always in the fire. She wanted the bankers out of the guild, and she had been gaining ground until she lost a supporter. She was not surprised the banker had been bought. She was surprised how many others had.

Jelfi was working the room, a gnome not much older than Facette, in a tiara of rubies, sapphires and emeralds. Astrid ritual cast Detect Magic and spent the ten minutes at a window, humming to the stars. Olo's beard net rang with enchantment. Soraya was wearing an invisible ring, right where Lucien had seen the dent. Jelfi gave off a powerful aura, and it was not a disguise. It was closer to an enhancement of herself. Astrid figured Jelfi had found a patron. With all that whispering into the night, she said, Facette should get one too.

Jelfi wanted to know how someone this handsome had been in her guild hall without her noticing. Milo told her he really wanted to get to know her, and cast Dispel Magic. The tiara cracked and fell off her head. Gems cracked in Olo's beard, and he looked woozy. Soraya's ring turned visible. Jelfi got older and grungier and lost her posture, and Milo knew the feeling. He pushed, and the rest of her magic came away. She began to smell of rotten eggs, and then she caught fire. She shoved him back. "Mammon, to me!" Two devils appeared, one with a beard of jeweled tentacles and one spined and slinking with a fistful of gold coins. Garran watched Olo the banker turn into a tall thing wrapped in chains.

Milo called up his Spirit Guardians as famous vampires, Lestat and Nosferatu among them. They burned radiant, since Milo has been tipping toward the good. Every enemy in the hall got caught in them, and Jelfi got the worst of it. Lucien's chromatic orb hit the spined devil, jumped to Jelfi and dropped her, then jumped again to the bearded one. The spined devil pelted Milo with gold coins and flew clear of the vampires. Astrid shot it dead. Garran marked the bearded devil and killed it in a single turn. Ralo went at the chained one and scared it, and it scared him right back. The two of them were terrified of each other. It hit him with its chains anyway.

Jelfi did not stay down. The broken tiara grew long spindly legs, clamped onto her skull, and tore her head off. Then her body got up without it. The head screamed from the floor, and Milo and Astrid were both terrified. She hurled fire at the two of them and put Milo down, and his vampires went with him. Pocket flew in and healed him. Garran finished off the chained devil, and Ralo was glad to see it go. He hated whatever it had been doing. Jelfi drew a scimitar made of shadow and left Ralo barely on his feet. Then he cut her down.

They found Kendrin alive in the cellar of Jelfi's shop. Jelfi had made a deal with a devil. It made her gems supernaturally good, and it let her sway whoever she gave them to. Tonight, on election night, she meant to crystallize Kendrin's heart and eat it, and keep his skill forever. Now a powerful gem cutter owes the crew some powerful favors. Ralo picked up her shadow scimitar. Mammon, the devil of greed, had given it to her.

The last we saw of Facette, she was back at her shop and still whispering into the night. She was etching Milo's likeness into an old gemstone, though she had only met him that morning. "You are the night. I whispered into the night, and you answered." A faint light came off her fingertips. Without meaning to, Milo had just granted somebody their very first cantrip. He felt his cheeks flush, and his hair held its coif a little better. We leave our scoundrels there, with a five-hundred-year-old vampire who had just become somebody's patron.
