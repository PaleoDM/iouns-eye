We opened on the sun deck with Lord Ondorel making a toast. He got business out of the way first, thanking our heroes for their work as security. Barran Hugentobler, a strong old family name that half the room pretended to recognize. The family was sailing for a tour of the globe after Farrowfield, so he encouraged every noble in the hall to rub elbows with Hugentobler while they still could. Then he gave a short wedding speech, introducing the groom: Dolphus Garner. The lad was Farrowfield grain money with the face of a soft nerd and the hands of a farmer.

Milo charmed the happy couple into letting him give a pre-wedding toast. Garran stayed to be seen staying and passed the forged case to Ralo on the way out. Ralo, Lucien and Astrid went below.

Astrid was one step from the door when Hedda Marchbank caught her arm. Hedda had not been paid, it was her first job out of the Abjurers Guild, and nobody had told her how any of that works. Her solution was to stay glued to Astrid until Garran paid everybody. Astrid failed the roll to shake her, and was caught temporarily with a tag along.

Ralo found the way down on instinct. They soon found a heavy door and two guards in front of it. Hedda spent that entire conversation studying the walls, then knocking on the hull to explain it was cork, because all the magic in the world will not make a ship light. They briefly considered bringing Hedda in, but then Astrid finally scared her off with Pocket. Ralo told the guards there was a woman down the hall knocking on the walls and asking about the construction, and off they went.

The door was barred from the far side, so there was nothing to pick. Astrid wild shaped into a scorpion and went under it. It wasn't really a vault, just the sturdiest corner of the hold. Astrid turned back into a woman and lifted the beam.

Milo's toast was not one toast; it was many. One for the beautiful women in the room, one for friendship, one for love, each separate, and each requiring the room to empty its glass, because it would be rude not to. He gave them in twenty two languages, which was how many were in the room, counting a few dialects of Firbolg and the regional undercommons. Milo worked the crowd through a long game of toasting and drinking and toasting and drinking. Somebody recognized the old wedding tradition from a story their grandmother told, and after that everyone decided he was simply very into history.

Ysvaine pulled Garran aside during one of them. She had been suspicious when Ottavia Brann brought him on, so she did her homework. Barran does not exist. No reputation, and a resume that began and ended three weeks ago. Meanwhile half the houses in that room had asked her to vouch for him. She was not angry. She was curious, and it compelled her. Garran lifted a bracelet off her wrist and let her see him do it, insisting that skills were resume enough. She told him to keep his secrets, and swapped places with the groom.

Twelve languages in, Garran clocked a woman crossing the floor with the precise intent of a killer. She was moving from the back toward the front, which meant toward the bride and groom, and toward Ondorel, and toward Milo. Garran went to head her off.

Below, Astrid had the parchment off Renzo Corvi's crew. One of Renzo's cousins knew a man in the Abjurers Guild, and they'd cased how to disable the wards. It took two on opposite sides of the case, and good thing they sent three. The case opened, and the real Evenbough was in front of them. Then Astrid caught the other thing in the room by a single point. A vault keeper, brass and mechanical and built like a minotaur, and there is no sequence for that.

Upstairs the woman tried to push past Garran and he got a hand on her. She rolled a natural one to break it, and a pistol came out. Initiative broke in both rooms at once.

Ralo charged the keeper and frightened it by shouting the ward sequence at it. Lucien opened with fire. Astrid used heat metal on it and cooked it from the inside every round after. The assassin could not shove free of Garran, so she levelled the pistol at the front of the room and fired. Milo took it under the collarbone mid-toast and his Bellini shattered in his hand. He was standing in front of the groom, so nobody could say which of them she had been shooting at. The bullet was silvered, and Milo could not help noticing how much worse that would have gone for him back when silver mattered to him.

Garran dragged her out of pistol range and ran her through with the rapier, and his aura frightened her. She told him the groom was a monster and that he had to let her go. Milo put Mirror Image on the groom, so there were three of him, and both of her disadvantaged shots took a duplicate. Someone punched the gun out of her hand, she came up once more with a dagger, and she was restrained. On a natural twenty, Milo sold the whole thing as part of the act.

Ralo turned his back on the keeper to hand the forgery to Astrid, ducked the opportunity attack, and got it across. Lucien took a crit, went prone under the thing, and beat the trample on a natural twenty. The keeper's nose ring fell open and it breathed poison gas, and Ralo, with almost nothing left, got low and let it rise past him. Then he put it down.

Astrid made the swap and had to hold her nerve doing it. The instrument remembers the hands that held it, and the last ones on it were Sylvara's, flush with everything she had been feeling in a room with Quarterstaff. Astrid choked it down. The Corvi parchment put the wards back up, and Mending made the keeper look like it had never moved.

They came back up into a hall still buzzing about the commotion, and one man still giving the longest speech in the history of Rifthaven. Ottavia Brann was crouched over the assassin at Garran's side. She turned the woman's wrist over and showed him a tattoo, a numeral three with each stroke made out of a stake. Some kind of monster hunting guild, and better kept between them than used to give away who the target had been. Then she inquired about future work. She was out of work at noon, and she asked whether he was hiring. Garran turned her down. She shook his hand and told him his crew was, unfortunately for her, a well-oiled machine.

For the finish Milo borrowed the groom's sword, commissioned from one of the most fabled artificers in Farrowfield, a gentleman by the name of Arlowe, to celebrate the agricultural renaissance following the siege of Farrowfield. Milo landed the dismount on a dirty twenty, and they walked off the ship with the real Evenbough and a couple of planks of Ondorel hardwood out of the vault. They also walked off with a name. The Third Act is a long-standing guild of monster hunters. They were aboard that morning, and they had come for the groom. Milo spent the walk home wondering who else in that room had been kindred.

Late on the twentieth Ralo went up to his new rooms and found Stella sitting with her back to him in front of his mirror, wearing nothing but the pearls that give her a human face. He had promised to come back and offer her something. Something by which to save herself from the Gatitos. A fortnight went by. So the family sent her with a message. One of his crew had gone into her mind, one of them had taken her pearls, and twice now Ralo had let a truce die. They were starting to wonder whether the da Pincis talk peace out of one side of their mouth while the other hand undercuts it. Then she turned around, and the hairpin was already in the air.

He saved against the worst of the poison and it still put him down. She crossed the room like a cat, put her claws to his cheek, and told him he should really go see DB before it was too late. The Gatitos had sent the message. The regards were DB's own. Then she reached back and unclasped the pearls, and the seam holding her together came apart, and what fell was a pile of rags.
