We opened with our heroes wrapping up with Edith on the afternoon of the 5th of Pelor. She offered them five gold apiece to find out who had shut her inside. She had not wanted to frighten the town, but someone had blocked her in. Indri asked where she kept her oven, since nobody bakes a pie without one. Edith said maybe she had a little magic of her own, and winked. It was a charm, about as strong as a friends cantrip, and Indri shook it off. She could not tell whether Edith knew she had done it. Edith asked whether her portrait had survived. Indri told her it was magic, and Edith said the only magic in it was decades of love. The twins are Edith and her brother, Asger. For a woman someone had tried to burn alive, she went back to sweeping up awfully fast.

They took a short rest at a picnic table, with Peetra passed out under the tree behind them. Yu wanted to cast spare the dying on him. He was not dying, just drunk, and not even hungover yet. They pooled what they had. A yellow lizard that flies is a dragon until proven otherwise.

Slate has blindsight now, and he followed the trail northeast to Fill's farm at the edge of town. The wheat was in for the winter. The house was dark at four in the afternoon, the back gate was swinging open, and someone had picked the front lock.

Harsk and Bringha crept to the door. Inside, things had been grabbed in a hurry, and a small figure crouched at an interior door, swearing at a set of lockpicks. Bringha got right up behind her and asked whether anyone had ever taught her to knock. It was Chippy. She lives there. She swung an elbow, recognized them, and decided they were spies. Then she started to cry. Fill was gone, everyone said he had set the fire, and he would never have left her.

Bringha kicked the door in. The door was strong and the hinges were not. Behind it was Fill's baking kitchen and the good oven, the one Chippy is not allowed to use until she makes journeyman. It looked like any other oven. Indri's detect magic found almost nothing in the house, only a faint trace on an empty rack over the mantel where a sword used to hang. Then a wisp of light slipped out between the oven bricks. Someone had built a hidden alcove into the back of it. Indri reached in and pulled out a freshly shed gold dragon scale, still warm. Harsk stuck his head in the oven and found a cone of scorch marks. Something small had been crawling in there to fire it.

Chippy had a story for that. Fill was a highway robber until a great hero named Laeryn killed all his friends and let him live, and he became a great hero too. Great heroes, she said, do not take in a girl and leave her the week she turns thirteen over a pie contest. She threw the scorched meat pie across the room. If he had abandoned her, she pointed out, the house was hers. Bringha gave her a job. Chippy is reading Fill's journals, and the password is strudel.

Another scale lay by the back gate. Harsk rolled a natural 20 and found a shortcut along an old stone wall. At the tree line stood Fill in armor, sword and shield, his back to them. Two bugbears were walking down out of the woods, long in the arm and the ear, speaking Goblin. Fill told them to keep a civil tongue, and asked them one thing. "Did my lad go this way?" One of them spat at him.

Harsk opened with a warning shot in Orcish and rolled a natural 1. The arrow sailed over Fill's head, and Fill raised his shield and assumed he was surrounded. Indri walked out into the open and asked him what was going on. He had a thirteen-year-old convinced he had abandoned her, and he appeared to own a dragon. Fill said he did not own him. Briochebane is his friend, and they were going to kill him. That was good enough for Indri, and she set a bugbear on fire. Magmin came running out of the woods to join in. Yu blessed the front line and ducked into a cart. One bugbear wrapped Fill up in its long arms and started dragging him away, and the other told the party their quarrel was not with townsfolk. Rasa threw a javelin with a smite behind it, and the earth came up through the bugbear's legs and held it. Fill followed the javelin back to its thrower and knew him. Rasa knocked one bugbear out, and Harsk put an arrow through the other one's eye. Slate heals from lightning, so Indri shocked him.

Fill swore he had not hurt Edith. Briochebane named himself, and he is the reason the meat pies are good. Something in the wood had lured him in, a Lord of the Fairfolk. Then a bigger bugbear stepped out of the trees in full armor, with a shield and a morningstar. The master wants the wyrm, it told Fill. They would take good care of him, the same way they take care of Asger. He would live forever. The rest of them could lay down their arms and go home.

Rasa was not going to allow that. Two more magmin ran out of the trees. Yu caught the big bugbear with a guiding bolt, and Harsk one-shot a magmin with another natural 20 and a lot of piss jokes. Bringha picked up the habit. A magmin told her that anything made of fire that makes piss jokes is one of them, and she punched it out. Fill shot the bugbear with a hand crossbow for two damage, his best of the night. It grabbed him to haul him off, but Rasa had called the earth up around its legs again. He did not spare this one. The earth climbed its arms and broke its grip, and his maul broke its neck. The earth held the body up afterward.

The bugbears rotted into mushrooms where they fell. Fill clasped Rasa's shoulder and said he owed Laeryn again, then told the rest of them the whole thing. As a young man he was a brigand. His crew tried to rob Laeryn's on the road, which was the worst decision of everyone's life but his. Laeryn let him live and told him to turn it around. About five years ago he tried adventuring, to be like him. His party killed giant rats in an old lady's cellar and worked up to real jobs, and one of those jobs was this wood. Two miles in there is a house that does not belong there. It is warm and cozy, and the cellar is full of the best food you can imagine. His whole party died there. In the larder he found a golden egg. He stayed in Culdir to keep watch on the wood, and last year Briochebane hatched. Fill knows nothing about being a father.

Fill hoped Briochebane had not burned down Edith's shop. Briochebane is about the size of a house cat, though, and a house cat does not push a cart against a door. Edith was inside when it happened, and nobody could work out how she missed it. Fill had seen the same fire spirits at her shop, and now that he knew they had a master, he did not like it. Then there was Asger. Fill had always heard that Edith's brother died in one of the last wars.

Fill offered them a strudel made the old-fashioned way, from before he had a dragon, and anything else he had if it would bring Briochebane home. Rasa told him he would answer for Briochebane's actions when this was over. Fill agreed. He would also have to tell Chippy that he had kept a baby dragon in the house the whole time. Bringha summed her up. "She's a real spitfire, that one." We leave our heroes dragging an unconscious bugbear back to Culdir, with a strudel coming.
