We opened with Olivier arriving at the new house with the aether crystal. He seemed especially interested in Astrid's parrots; asking if they were the mysterious "Passenger Parrots" and inquiring if they were truly extinct. "Apparently not" Astrid chided, seemingly uninterested. The exposition landed, though. The city is abuzz with news that the *Meridian Queen* is returning to Rifthaven. She'll be in port a fortnight before departing on another circuit of Astoria.

As Olivier departed, Quill Bill came in with a little rhyme and suggested they check out the little black book, reminding them all of a job they had seen that mentioned the *Meridian Queen*. 

Our scoundrels parted through a lot of the Serpent's hubbub quickly and distilled it down to the core of it. The score is the Evenbough, House Ondorel's lute, and it sails with the family when the *Meridian Queen* leaves. To pull this off, they'd need a decoy. They went back and forth on where one comes from. Sylvara, who they do not know but appears to be a neighbor here in Inkwell, or someone out of Milo's past. They settled on the fact that Milo's contact, if they exist, probably could not make it magical, and they were worried about Milo needing to show face and be known to someone. So they decided to go check out Sylvara anyway.

Lucien noted he found this particular job a little debasing and was not thrilled about the prospect of selling it. Who is going to buy an instrument with a reputation like this? Then Ralo did some investigating and found moon runes in the black book. The Serpent had already sourced a buyer. The Orlovs, a rival noble family with a deep hatred for the Ondorels, who would almost certainly pay for the instrument just to flaunt it in the Ondorels' faces. The Serpent had already taken care of fencing it down the line.

They came out of the exchange deciding that Sylvara was a civilian, and that she needed to be conned herself for this to work. So only two of them went in, Lucien and Milo, and both went in disguise. Milo went as Frederick Quarterstaff, a famous conductor of the Glamour District Symphony Orchestra. Lucien went as its first chair, Alon Bardoof.

Quarterstaff set about convincing Sylvara that Bardoof is an objectum sexual, someone romantically attracted to and involved with objects, in this case lutes. Bardoof had recently broken up with his previous lute and was likely never to play again unless he could find an instrument of equal majesty and beauty. Something irresistible. It was very funny and entirely above board, and Milo crushed the rolls, 25 and up. Sylvara came out of it very smitten with Quarterstaff and very dedicated to finding Bardoof his new lute. She also told them the lute would be on display that night at a fancy gala, and that she thought Quarterstaff would be conducting it.

A plan was hatched. Ralo would play Quarterstaff's driver, which let him ferry Sylvara around and keep eyes on Quarterstaff. They bought Sylvara a very expensive ticket that put her close to the action. The rest of the crew would arrive at the gala with one goal, collecting the eight pieces of information Sylvara needs to make the duplicate. The type of wood, the sound it makes, the maker's mark, the school of magic, the wear pattern, any inscription, the feeling it gives when you touch it, and the case itself.

On the night of the 6th of Pelor they arrived. Astrid positioned herself as a cocktail waiter so she could quickly distribute communications to the group. Garran infiltrated backstage security, both to make sure nothing untoward went down and to get access to the restricted areas. Ralo disguised himself as a portraitist. Milo went as Quarterstaff.

They had the real Quarterstaff derailed before the gala entirely, which meant Milo was on the hook for performing the duties of a conductor. And boy did he. Milo's rolls were fantastic, and it turns out Milo has very extensive experience being a conductor, even melding styles from different schools of conducting. That side of things went off completely without a hitch.

Lucien came in as himself, since Bardoof was not playing this particular event, which gave him flexibility. He identified the first chair for the evening right away, a very young and nervous halfling man for whom this was clearly the biggest opportunity of his life. Lucien charmed him, suggested a bite to eat would calm his nerves, and took him back toward the kitchen. Then Lucien spilled soup on him. Astrid swooped in at that exact moment with a calming tonic and took the jacket away to be pressed and cleaned. The tonic left the halfling on a chamber pot for several hours, completely out of commission. Lucien slotted in as first chair with a Disguise Self.

Having Sylvara in the front row while the lute was on display and being played checked off three or four of the eight on its own.

Then Ralo clocked something. A member of the cocktail band was actually a rival scoundrel pulling a con. It was Renzo "Basta" Corvi, and Ralo recognized him as the killer of Lonnie "Lobster" da Pinci. Ralo told us here that Lonnie had been sleeping with Corvi's wife. What we had previously thought was a crime between the two families was in fact a crime of passion, and it has complicated the relationship between those families ever since. The plot thickens.

Ralo passed the information to Astrid, who got it to Garran, and that was pivotal. It let Garran run interference long before Corvi ever got close or anything could pop off.

The performance ended and there was an opportunity for a portrait with the instrument. Quarterstaff and the disguised Lucien began posing, and then Quarterstaff made a huge kerfuffle about there being too much masculine energy in the portrait, and about it needing a feminine figure and feminine hands. With that he manufactured a scenario for Sylvara to be in the portrait and put hands on the instrument. Ottavia Brann, head of security, was not impressed until Milo talked her down.

That was the same moment Corvi tried to make a play for the instrument himself. Garran intervened, and because of the crowd he did not immediately go to violence. He used his fear aura to back Corvi down, kept him from moving closer to the instrument, and eventually got him around a corner and fully intimidated into leaving.

They reconvened with seven of the eight pieces of information. The one outstanding was the magical aura. Garran took the opportunity to pretend to be security sweeping for bugs and cast Detect Magic. It got them the eighth piece and it extremely impressed Ottavia Brann, who offered Garran a spot on the extra hired security in two weeks' time, when the instrument gets loaded onto the *Meridian Queen*. Note that Brann does not know Garran as Garran. She knows him as Barran.

All in all, an extreme success. We leave our scoundrels with all eight pieces, a further in for the next leg of the job, and Sylvara a little bit smitten with Quarterstaff. Sooner or later it may get out that the real Quarterstaff was never there.
