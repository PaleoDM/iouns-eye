Session 0 was character creation and shared worldbuilding. No in-fiction events. The notes below capture what we landed on for the campaign and for each character, so we walk into Session 1 already knowing who we are and the world we share.

Session 1 opens on **22 Ioun 1130 TA** — high summer, in the King Tides window — roughly one year after the end of *Rifthaven: Root of Rebellion*.

## The Campaign in One Line

> *The upper class as viewed by criminals, rather than the lower class as viewed by heroes.*

You are professional scoundrels — fixers, specialists, unseen hands — working jobs in Rifthaven against nobles, merchants, politicians, and institutions. Targets are no longer monsters or Rift abominations. Failure looks like jail, debt, exile, or a knife in an alley. The previous Rifthaven campaign pushed *up* from below. This one operates *inside* a world it does not belong in, and steals from it.

The city is still shaped by the revolution of the previous campaign. The world has continuity. You do not.

## The Party

### Milo Lafite-Margeaux — Human Lore Bard

Nearly five centuries old. Originally a wealthy human merchant turned vampire, who rose to head of a vampire crime syndicate and made his living conning marks with supernatural charm. Then he got conned himself, and was cured of his vampirism against his will. His clan excommunicated him out of pity. He still identifies as a vampire and is desperate to return to that life. He is down bad about the realization that nobody actually liked him — they liked the supernatural charisma. Walking in sunlight for the first time in centuries is a quiet daily nightmare.

**Heartbreak:** Centuries ago, a famous opera singer turned him down despite his charm. She is elderly now but still an absolute queen, and he is still down bad for her.

### Garran — Human Hollow Warden Ranger

Freelance mercenary. Reputation: gets the job done. Surly and silent, says nothing about his past. Treats business as business. Dexterous and acrobatic, smart, doesn't suffer fools, no aspiration to climb ranks.

**Dangerous debt:** Walked away from a job because the employer was dishonest about the terms. Ambiguous whether he walked for morals or for the principle of having been misled. Either way, there is a bounty on his head for the unfinished work.

Garran is the keeper of the thieves' code: **stick to the plan, always communicate.**

### Ralo Battaglia — Halfling Battle Master Fighter

Mid-level goon in the da Pinci crime syndicate. Quick to give people nicknames they hate. Jovial. Content with mid-level status. Secretly has a big heart, but absolutely does shady, unscrupulous things.

**Family:** Son RJ (Ralo Junior). In a trial separation from RJ's mother that is going badly. His whole family resents him for not climbing the ladder, and everyone wants a bigger piece of his pie. His safe house is his elderly mother's apartment — she gives him constant grief but takes him in.

**Pressure:** His former boss, **Lonnie "Lobster" da Pinci**, was recently killed. Lonnie had been paying off the Aletheia Procedure for protection. When those payments stopped, his rivals took him out. The pressure is now on Ralo to step up and fill the gap — pressure he does not want.

### Lucien — Human Wild Magic Sorcerer

Calm, well-dressed, mild-mannered. Doesn't raise his voice. Originally a white-collar fixer for criminals — forging documents, producing blackmail. While fixing a set of genealogy documents for a client family, he opened an ancient scroll in a catacomb and was struck by a surge of wild magic.

His magic is **not Fey, not elemental** — it is procedural and contractual in nature. He uses it the way he used to use his legal proclivities. Resents being treated as a tool; preferred being a person for hire.

**The Ashen Barristers:** His first job after his awakening was working for a private legal society of lawyers, administrators, notaries, and clerks — in their magical writ-tampering unit. They might still help him in a bind, if it came to that.

### Astrid — Human Stars Druid

**Official assignment:** protect a herd of displacer beasts from poachers during their rare planar migration to the Material Plane to breed. Migration is roughly every sixteenth moon, so she has significant free time.

**Official side job:** breeds chinchillas and toucans as messenger animals — critical to the crew's job logistics.

**Unofficial job:** demolition expert. Her brand of demolition involves sneaking in via Wild Shape and destroying things as an animal.

A cryptid conspiracy theorist with a list she desperately hopes to prove real. Cool with basically all crimes — except poaching.

**Regret:** Once on a job, she had to abandon a real chance at concrete evidence of a cryptid she was desperately interested in. The job came first. She still loses sleep over it.

## What We Established About the World

- **The Forger's Magitech.** A piece of magitech dragged up from the Rift forges handwriting perfectly. The entire criminal underbelly of Rifthaven is buzzing about it.
- **The Rival Crew.** Another crew — never much of a rival before — has suddenly found itself funded by a wealthy benefactor, and is now snatching up the best jobs.
- **Lonnie "Lobster" da Pinci is dead.** His protection arrangement with the Aletheia Procedure lapsed. The da Pinci syndicate is unstable in his absence.
- **The Thieves' Code:** *Stick to the plan, always communicate.* The unwritten rule of the underworld. Critical glue, especially given how dependent the crew is on Astrid's animal messengers.
- **The Sparkworks District is avoided.** Rumor says it's full of do-gooder heroes — the heroes of the previous Rifthaven campaign. Running jobs there is a recipe for getting whacked.
