We opened with our heroes stepping into Sallow's lair. Vicas read off a scroll of Bless. Combat was on.

Vicas rolled top of initiative and charged straight in. He was nearly out of focus points and he knew it. He told the party, plainly, that he was going to race Sallow to the end. Lyra came in just behind him, Dawn's Radiance flaring, stacking onto Vicas's damage. Thump hit with a heavy Grasping Vine. Rooker dropped Evard's Black Tentacles to lock things down and feed Azrael more openings. The race was on.

Then Sallow answered.

He hit the party with a powerful flame burst. Azrael and Vicas both ducked it entirely thanks to Evasion. The other three weren't so lucky. Lyra, Thump, and Rooker took the hit hard and came out of it on fire. Round two went almost entirely to damage control. Lyra patted herself out. Thump put Rooker out. Rooker, thanks to a quirk of initiative, returned the favor on Thump. Burning to death wasn't going to be how this ended, but the round had been spent. Meanwhile, Vicas and Azrael kept pouring in damage, knowing exactly what their job was.

Round three, things got back on track. Vicas spent his last focus point and announced it to the party. From here on, it was just regular old damage. That was when Sallow, badly bloodied now, pulled out his final big spell. Chain Lightning. Rooker reached for a counterspell. Sallow burned a legendary save and let it fly. The bolt arced through the whole party. Azrael had already been dropped the round before. The Chain Lightning put Lyra, Thump, and Rooker on the ground with him. Vicas was the only one left standing. He had no focus left to heal.

But Vicas is fast. And Lyra had a healing potion on her belt.

He sprinted to her, poured the potion down her throat, and got her back up. Lyra dropped a Mass Healing Word and the rest of the party came back online. Thump layered his own healing on top. In a single round, the fight flipped.

They converged on Sallow. Vicas finished it. He drove his spear up through Sallow's chin and out the other side, tearing away part of his face, and shoved the body through a gap in the juggernaut's chassis. Sallow fell. He landed at the feet of the trapped juggernaut, impaled vertically on the same caltrop-pike Hrothgar had used to root the thing in place.

Our heroes cleaned up. Potions and prayers went around the room until everyone was on their feet. Thump approached the heart of the chamber and reclaimed the seed of the Myreshade. The juggernaut's promise was kept. The seed was his now, and so was the duty that came with it.

And then we turned to our heroes' epilogues.

We saw Thump return to the Myreshade. He found a place no one else would find, deep in the heart of the forest, and he planted the seed. He didn't take over the Timbermaster's post, but he stood beside it, advising the new druids he gathered around him, helping them learn to listen to the wood the way he'd learned to. The Myreshade grew back. Not the way it was. Better.

We saw Lyra back on the road. Lady Rowena set her to a long task: bring the people of Ekkorai together under one banner. So she went. Firewatch Island. The Vegepygmies. The logging camps and every quiet community along the way. The work she'd always done, only now with even more weight behind it.

We saw Rooker hang up his adventuring hat. He helped found an arcane school with Headmistress Sombra at its head and Meris as one of its key archivists. Magical research, properly funded and properly housed. A second campus was planned in the Red Vale to take in the Vegepygmy children with the gift.

We saw Azrael travel with Th0l, gathering intelligence on the forces still moving against Ishetar. The two of them, with Vicas's help, set their sights on the Temple of the Celestial Seed. They meant to bring it down, and to rescue anyone still trapped inside.

Vicas walked with Azrael for a while, doing what he'd always done. Then he went home. He spent years walking the trails Shallah had walked, retracing her old stories, seeing the places where her memory lingers. He got to know her in the way he hadn't been able to in life.

And as his own years waned, Vicas came back to Ishetar. He sat down beneath the maple tree, at the grave he'd once asked Thump, painfully, to prepare for him. He made himself a cup of tea. He opened his veins to the roots and went quietly, the way he'd always meant to, to find Shallah.

And as Vicas's story fades out, so too does our time in Ishetar.
