We opened by settling what Sylvara thinks she is doing. She has all eight pieces and ten days to build the copy, and she will do it well. As far as she knows she is building a lute for Alon Bardoof, who broke up with his last one and will not play again until he finds its equal. The crew decided she does not need to be cut in, warned, or cleaned up after. She is a patsy who does not know it, and the worst thing waiting for her is a broken heart.

Two days after the gala, Ottavia Brann came to Barran. She knows two things about that night. Someone tried to steal the Evenbough, and someone impersonated Frederick Quarterstaff. She does not know those were two different crews.

Brann put 250 gold in Garran's hand up front and told him to put together a team. The job is Renzo Corvi. Bring him in, alive if it can be managed, because House Ondorel would very much like to ask him some questions. Brann does not know he already has a crew, or that he was standing in a room with all of them.

She offered leads. The real Quarterstaff, whoever booked the cocktail band, the kitchen staff. Ralo had one Brann did not, since he knows Corvi killed Lonnie over his wife. Go find Mrs. Corvi.

There was talk about whether Quarterstaff needed handling. Lucien argued the correct play is to do nothing at all. A man cannot publicly claim he did not conduct a performance the whole city is still raving about, and the broadsheets keep running his quotes praising his own work. What's the charge, conducting a succulent orchestral symphony? They left him alone and went to see Stella instead.

Stella keeps rooms above a nightclub out on one of the decks that sit over the rift. The place stopped being fashionable about fifteen years ago and nobody has told it yet. Ralo went in a zoot suit. Milo dressed to the nines. Astrid did not understand what clubbing was and arrived in her fieldwork coveralls, which went so far past wrong that it came back around, and people spent the night trying to work out her whole deal.

Ralo walked up to the half-orc bartender and ordered a cosmopolitan. She asked if it was for him. He ordered two. Something about that put three or four of the rougher men in the room back on their heels. She slid a bottle rack aside to show a staircase and told him the mistress would be expecting him.

Upstairs was old money going quietly to seed. Stella took one look, ushered everyone else in, and left Ralo standing in the hall. "Batty fucking gee, you got a lot of nerve showing your face around here."

They have history. She knows about DB. She got a good long look at Astrid and asked Ralo to tell her it wasn't like that. He insisted, of course, that it wasn't. Ralo pivoted and asked how attached she is to her husband.

Then she reached back and unclasped her pearls, and the glamour came off with them. Stella Corvi was born Stella Gatito, a clouded leopard tabaxi, third daughter of a second aunt. Renzo married into the family and knew the terms going in. A gal has a job to do for the people who pay for the lifestyle. "When you think about it like that, Ralo, I'm just a working class dame, selling my labor for my lifestyle."

She was never sleeping with Lonnie. Not yet. She was working him. The Gatitos and the da Pincis were close to a truce and Lonnie was the way in, and Renzo did something emotional and killed the whole thing along with the man. Ralo has known since last session that this was a crime of passion. He did not know it was also the murder of a peace deal.

Lucien had subtle cast Detect Thoughts before any of that, so she never knew a spell went off in her house. On the surface she was doing arithmetic. What does Ralo want, and how much can I squeeze him for it. Game recognizing game. When he pushed deeper she failed the save. She has already made peace with the fact that her husband is going to die. She knew about the lute job and had tried to talk him out of it, because it was never going to work. There was a flash of a fish market. And underneath it, one thing she wants so badly it drowns everything else out. Ralo has to broker the peace himself. Nothing less will do.

She felt the probe and called it, and the room got sharp. Lucien put it to her plainly. A lot of people are looking for her husband, none of them know she wasn't part of it, and when she tells them so they are not going to believe her. She held his eye and folded. The Lamplighter Market, an old fishing market, and she gave the district, the corner and the streets. Don't come crawling back if he's moved on.

On the way out Astrid lifted the pearl necklace off the serving tray.

Ralo hung back. Different woman in the hallway. She put a hand on his forearm, claws and all, and told him how the week goes. In a couple of days the family comes to see how she has been doing. Renzo will probably be dead by then, and she will throw him under the bus and pin every bit of it on him to save herself. After that it goes one of two ways. They cut her loose, and the crew has lost a powerful ally. Or they show her mercy, and the crew has made an enemy of the Gatitos. She does not think either one is good for them.

Then she asked after Batty Junior, who is getting tall these days. Ralo insisted that he's lazy, and spends all his time with his shithead uncles. Stella told Ralo she'd tell DB he was still thinking about her.

They went down to the Lamplighter Market that night. It is not a nightlife district and it was dark, and they got further in than they meant to. Shuttered stalls, canals running through, nobody sure whether the man moving crates across the way was a civilian or a Gatito. Then lanterns on the far bank, men playing cards, voices carrying over the water. One of them saying they should bail on the job. Another saying that if they go to ground the family will cut them out. Fifteen or twenty feet of canal between them, a couple of plank bridges, and no interest in doing this quietly.

The first man across recognized them. "It's them from last night." He put a Witchbolt into Astrid, and the tether pinned him in the open, since breaking line would have dropped the spell. Astrid asked Ralo for permission to use the gun. She got it. Starry form, an arrow, then the pistol, and the man realized a little late that he had opened a firefight with a gunsmith.

Corvi had a bad night. Milo hit him with Hold Person and he rolled a natural 20 on the save, so Milo spent inspiration to make him roll again, and his own luck bought him a third. He failed anyway. Later Milo hit him with Tasha's Hideous Laughter, and then hit him with it a second time. Lucien dropped a Web across the crossing, then threw a firebolt into his own web, rolled a single point of damage, and set the whole thing burning. One of Corvi's men burned to death in it.

Garran held the bottleneck and took nearly all of it until Astrid stepped off the line to put him back together. Pocket worked every round. Ralo frightened the heavy with a menacing attack and then spent the rest of the fight with three men swinging on him. Milo spent his cutting words on "get bent" and "I would never invite you to a party."

The Corvi Five went down. They had Renzo surrounded and every right to finish it, and took him alive instead. Fifty gold more from Brann for the trouble, and the goodwill of the woman running security on the thing they actually came to steal.

They came away with a Cloak of Elvenkind off Corvi's back, which went to Garran, and Astrid's stolen pearls turned out to be a Necklace of Disguise. And the best of it. Corvi's crew had been casing the same job as long as the party has, and they were carrying something about the security around the Evenbough that our scoundrels would never have turned up on their own.

We leave our scoundrels paid, holding a prisoner who can tell Ottavia Brann that he never wore Quarterstaff's face, with Stella upstairs in a seedy nightclub working out how to survive the week. Eleven days until the *Meridian Queen* sails.
