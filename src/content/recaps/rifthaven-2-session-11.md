We opened on the far side of the six-faced doors, in the Ironclad District. Nobody in the party can see in the dark, so they went by torchlight. Zed had no map, just his father's directions from the central washing well. The district held up better than anyone expected. A couple hundred years is nothing to dwarven stonework. The road ran three wagons wide past a customs house and rows of empty shops. Garran took point and moved the party from cover to cover under Pass Without Trace. Nobody knew of anything down here to hide from, and he hid them anyway.

Alongside the road ran a mine-cart track with fifteen feet of rail torn straight up, and past that a shop with its door frame clawed open. Astrid had no idea what did it, so she went to her list of cryptids and settled on the Saltpeter Cat. It is a giant feral cat drawn to demolition sites, and she has blurry evidence.

Inside, in an old chandlery, a handful of dwarves had made a last stand. Two died by the claw. The rest had been eaten down to bare bone by acid. There was a dwarf carved in stone behind the counter.

Ralo found another last stand across the street. It put him in mind of the six-faced doors, which had been locked from the outside. Somebody had been keeping something in.

Garran got cocky on a double natural twenty and took a shortcut, and it brought them out at the central square beside another statue. A dwarf held his little girl to his chest with one hand and covered her eyes with the other. He had a real coin purse on his belt with sixty-two gold inside, and Astrid lifted it with mage hand. Zed told them he had known some fantastic masons, but none this good. Astrid figured they had been turned to stone while trying not to look at something.

The party asked Zed if dwarves tell stories about things that live in wells. He had fifteen thousand of them, mostly creepy little girls, long hands, tongue tentacles and things that eat your heart. He had warned them upfront that he would be useless.

An axe beak came around the corner by the well, huge and starving. It had no claws, so Astrid ruled it out for the shop. Ralo caught a stone-colored tail at the edge of the torchlight, one of a pair of basilisks.

Garran cast Jump, cleared the washing well in one bound, and put two rapier hits into the bird. It answered with a crit. The first basilisk turned its blue eyes on Ralo, and he stared right back and passed. It bit him for his trouble. The second caught Milo and Astrid in its gaze, and they both went sluggish, the first step toward stone. Astrid spent the last of her bad luck from Ioun on a missed shot. Pocket helped her shake it off, and Milo walked it off.

Milo's dissonant whispers hit the note the axe beak's old mate used to sing, and it ran.

Lucien threw a lightning bolt through the bird and one basilisk, and his magic surged. For the next hour, anything that killed him would bring him straight back. He walked up to the front line and stayed there.

Garran leapt back across the well and killed the first basilisk. The second slowed him to half speed. He jumped twenty-five feet anyway, on the argument that it was a squat and not a walk, and killed that one too. The axe beak came back and got its beak into Lucien. Then Astrid's musket went off, one very mechanical bang in a fight full of spells, and put a slug through its skull.

Zed got his bearings and led them to a row of stone houses built into the cavern wall. His family crest was worked into the iron gate, a whiskey barrel over two crossed hammers, for the Malthammers. Most of the house had caved in. Zed wanted the cellars fast, because the whiskey draws treasure hunters and predators alike.

Ralo found a coal chute off the old kitchen. It was too tight for armor, so he stripped down to his weapons. At the bottom he smelled aged wood, whiskey, and lamp oil.

He tried to push up through the rotten kitchen floor from below and couldn't manage it. Nobody wanted explosives near that much lamp oil. So Astrid turned into a very fat black bear, fell straight through the floor, and landed on Ralo. Everyone else climbed down after.

A short tunnel led down into a room that was half family crypt and half whiskey cellar. There were ten kegs. Some were whiskey, and the rest were decoys full of oil, laid down to punish smugglers. The only test Zed knew was Churro's nose, one keg at a time. The deal stood. The first real keg went to the Malthammers, the second was theirs at 250 gold, and every keg after that was gold or a favor from the church. Milo found the coffins and considered a nap.

The moment they stepped in, two ankhegs came up spitting acid, and the far coffin opened. Something had eaten its occupant long ago. Two umber hulks climbed out, all shell and spinning kaleidoscope eyes. Ralo was still out of his armor.

Ralo parked Churro at the nearest keg and waited. Garran slipped an arrow between an ankheg's plates, and it sprayed acid over him and Milo. The first umber hulk turned its eyes on the room, and Garran and Milo came away confused. The second went for Lucien three times. Lucien put up Mirror Image and a Shield, and nothing landed.

Milo was confused, and he acted anyway by burning two charges of his mind sharpener. He caught an ankheg and both umber hulks in Hypnotic Pattern. It was a flash of his old vampiric glamour, like looking into the sun used to feel. All three locked up where they stood, and the fight became one monster at a time.

Astrid did not fire the musket. She has a twelve intelligence, which is plenty to keep black powder out of a room full of lamp oil. She fought in starry form instead and lit targets up with guiding bolts for everyone else. Churro worked the room one keg at a time. The keg by the door was whiskey, left there for a quick nip. The next three were oil.

Lucien stayed in reach of the umber hulks the whole fight, and burned through his shields and then his duplicates. Ralo got confused and lost a turn to it. Garran put Hunter's Mark on the first umber hulk and dropped it with a crit. Milo woke the second one up on purpose. It finally got a real hit on Lucien, and Milo finished it with a starry wisp. Ralo and Garran put down the last ankheg.

Afterward, Churro finished the count. Four of the ten kegs were real. The first went to Zed and the second was worth 250 gold. The party still has to decide whether the last two become gold or favors from the church. Milo has suggested cracking one open with Zed's family first, for a friendlier negotiation.

Ralo put his armor back on. They hauled four kegs of Malthammer whiskey out of the Ironclad and home to the GoldSoul, and that was the end of the whiskey business. We leave our scoundrels there for now, until the next job finds them.
