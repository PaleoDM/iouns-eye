## Description

An old fishing market on **Gatito** turf, out where the canals run. Shuttered stalls that open in the mornings, plank bridges over fifteen or twenty feet of water, warehouses along the banks. Not a nightlife district. After dark it is easy to get further in than you meant to and lose your bearings.

**Renzo "Basta" Corvi** kept a safe house here.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 7

**Stella Corvi** gave up the location under pressure, down to the district, the corner and the streets, with no guarantee her husband would still be there.

He was. The crew found the **Corvi Five** playing cards by lantern light on the far bank, arguing about whether to bail on the job and whether the family would backstab them if they went to ground.

The fight that followed killed four of them. **Lucien** set his own Web burning with a firebolt and one of Corvi's men burned to death in it. Corvi himself spent most of it paralyzed and then laughing on the ground, and the crew took him alive.
