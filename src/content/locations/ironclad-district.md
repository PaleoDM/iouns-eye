## Description

An old dwarven district buried deep in the rift, sealed off two centuries ago when the rift itself swallowed it whole. **Zedediah**'s family fled topside in the collapse, leaving behind their ancestral home and a stash of aging family whiskey. A fresh tremor cracked the tunnels back open, exposing them to looters and treasure hunters, unlicensed to enter without a permit from the treasure hunters' guild and patrolled from above by Griffin-mounted customs wardens.

The gates into the district proper are a pair of massive stone doors carved with six divine faces, requiring an offering to each of six ancient gods in calendar order to open. Beyond them lies a fully intact stretch of underground dwarven city: roads wide enough for wagons three abreast, ceilings lost somewhere in the dark.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 10

**Zedediah** led the crew down into the rift to recover his family's sealed whiskey, guided by his whistle-whisker **Churro**. The approach cost a stolen air skiff, a freefall off the edge of the rift, a talked-down customs inspection, and a fight against a cockatrice and a pair of giant vultures guarding the cave mouth. Inside, the crew solved the six-faced door by offering wine, forged art, a lucky coin, a favorite law, field notes, and a toast to the six oldest prime deities, in the order of the calendar. The doors opened onto the district's main road, and that's where the session ended.
