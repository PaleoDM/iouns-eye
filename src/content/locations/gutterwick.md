## Description

The flooded slums at the tideline of north Rifthaven, where class is made literal as elevation — the poor live where the water reaches. Sorvellan tenements go ankle-deep at high water. Not a place to walk careless after dark.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 2

The crew followed **Lady Hypatra**'s lead down into Gutterwick to find **Wrenna Tidwell**, sister of **La Marginalia**. La Marginalia is said to have a safe house somewhere in the district, where she went to ground after being wounded.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 3

The crew fought through the district at night to reach that safe house — a bolt-hole beneath the **Almshouse**, the beloved relief house the locals protect so fiercely the Sorvellan rent-men trailing the crew would not follow them in. The district's poverty is the backdrop to the whole game: it is **House Sorvellan**'s squeezed slum rent that funds the family, and the same block that shelters **Maggie**.
