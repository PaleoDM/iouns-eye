## Description

Rifthaven's print and publishing ward, where the city's broadsheets and pamphlets are set, run, and bound. The presses work night shifts, so the row stays awake and lamplit after midnight. News that comes off a Folio Row press is news the city believes, which is exactly why the Sorvellan manifesto was queued to run here.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 4

The crew came to a Folio Row press to stop the manifesto reaching print. They found a **Tabula Rasa** agent already leaning on the printer, scared him off, then conned their way inside and walked out with every broadsheet and the linocut plate.
