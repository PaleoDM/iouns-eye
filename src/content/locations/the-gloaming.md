The dark, ancient forest at the edge of Glancoed, named in the common tongue for where the light dies. Villagers have long spotted strange cold lights flickering deep among its trees, and the wood is thick with ambient magic that bleeds off the wreck of an old giant-wrought machine somewhere in its heart.

Lately the wood has gone wrong. A blight the villagers call *the sickening* is spreading out from its depths, and the animals now refuse the treeline. Eirlys, the village witch, walked in three days before the travelers arrived to find the source of it, and she never came back. As of the end of Session 1, the party has agreed to go in after her.

## Inside the Wood

The party crossed into the Gloaming in Session 2 and found it worse the deeper they went. Fewer birds, then fewer signs of anything living at all. Both the water and the air are poisoned, which makes clean water the single most valuable thing anyone can carry in there. The trees themselves can split open and belch out sulfurous gas, so lingering is dangerous and lingering where the gas seeps is worse. The stream running past Rhydderch's camp was supposed to be one of the last clean sources left, and it now runs red with a sooty staining along its edges.

A few hours in there is a clearing full of ancient giant technology, scattered and half-sunk into the ground. The dead there have been raised by something that is part necromancy and part still-running Second Age arcanotech, and they carry a fear aura.

Upstream stands the great alder: split open down its length and pouring red poison into the water. It had stood for centuries deliberately drawing the poison out of the wood around it and holding it in its own bark, taking the sickness so nothing else had to. A pair of snakes made it worse on purpose to lure Eirlys to it.

## The Source of the Sickening

The alder gave Indri a memory that answered the question the village has never been able to. A few hours north of the alder, through the untamed part of the wood where no path goes, lies the carcass of **Death at Sunset**, a green dragon brought down centuries ago by five giant-wrought flying longboats. The scattered wreckage in the wood is what remains of the four that shattered against her. Her venom, in that quantity, left where it fell, has been seeping outward ever since. The sickening has one source, and it is her body.
