## Description

A working-class bath house in Rifthaven that services rough laborers on their way home from a hard shift, with a bar and smokes for the ones who don't rush off. Run by the tiefling **Cobalt**, who keeps it as neutral ground in the city's turf wars. The back rooms are available for private meetings — *if* you know to pay the house for them.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 1

The whole crew converged here for different reasons — some chasing a job, some there on other business — and met their first patrons, the **Sorvellans**, who hired them to recover Vendris's compromising documents. The Sorvellans' failure to pay for their private room nearly turned into a brawl with Cobalt's bouncers before it was talked down. The party closed the night here over a game of cards.
