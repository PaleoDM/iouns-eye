## Description

A Rifthaven institution of divination magic. As of Session 1 it is bleeding members — enough turnover that newly accepted students have had their places deferred.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 1

**Celeste** mentioned in passing that one of the College's professors has gone missing, and that the resulting turnover has delayed her own accepted place. A standing personal hook for **Astrid**, whose divination is the genuine article.
