A small village on the neck of the peninsula, pronounced KIL-deer. The road south runs through the neck, and Culdir is the one real stop on it. It is smaller than Glancoed and does well off the traffic.

Culdir is famous for a battle. The giants marched an army of pressed small folk through the neck, led by very few giants. Two elemental titans, Voshgaroth of storm and wind and Morosko of cold, set their squabbles aside to stop it, just this once. The giants' army still stands frozen in the north field, and the neck has been bitter cold ever since. Locals call it the unpleasantness out on the North Field.

## Hearth and Hatchlight, Session 5

The party arrived on the 5th of Pelor, 2184 SA, in the middle of a midwinter festival. Culdir was one of the only towns left with sweets, thanks to Edith Applegarth, and recruiters were working the green. Edith won the pie contest for the fifty-fifth year running. That afternoon her shop caught fire with her barricaded inside, and the party pulled her out.

Notable folk: **Edith Applegarth**, the sweet-maker; **Fill Flourforge**, the baker; **Chippy**, his apprentice; and **Peetra Sylver**, who drinks under a tree and sees more than people think.

## Hearth and Hatchlight, Session 6

Fill's farm sits at the edge of town, wheat fields put away for the winter. Behind it, a path runs northeast along an old stone wall to the tree line. Two miles into that wood there is a house that does not belong there, and whatever lives in it sends bugbears and fire spirits out of the trees. The party dragged one bugbear back to Culdir alive, for the stocks.
