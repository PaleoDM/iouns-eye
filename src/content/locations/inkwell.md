## Description

A bohemian quarter of **Rifthaven**, and now home to the crew's first real property. As near-payment for the second half of the Sorvellan job, **Vendris Sorvellan** signed over the deed to a shophouse here, a gift from his late mother and the one asset he could put his own name to. What a crew of scoundrels makes of the place remains to be seen.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 4

The crew took the Inkwell deed to close out **The Manifesto**, with Vendris still owing a further hundred gold on top of it. We leave them some days later walking up on the shophouse for the first time.
