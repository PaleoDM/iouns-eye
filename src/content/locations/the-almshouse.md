## Description

A legitimate relief house in **Gutterwick** — flood relief, a breadline, a roof for the district's poorest — and also the cover for **La Marginalia**'s bolt-hole, hidden beneath it. The locals protect the place fiercely enough that even the Sorvellan rent-men working the block will not set foot inside; they are not welcome, and they know it. A stern matron keeps the door.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 3

**Wrenna Tidwell** led the crew here through the night, a tail of rent collectors trailing them the whole way but unwilling to follow into the Almshouse itself. The matron grilled the scoundrels hard and made them earn entry; only on Wrenna's word did she relent, pour them wine, and send them down to **Maggie** in the bolt-hole below.
