## Description

The customs house on the way out of one of **Rifthaven**'s fancy districts. Anything leaving under ward stops here to be declared. The wards come down, the officials check the contents against the manifest, the manifest gets stamped, and the wards go back up. The whole thing takes about two minutes, and somebody official has now watched the goods leave the district, which matters as much for the insurance as for the theft.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 8

The first of the three danger points on the Evenbough's run. **Astrid** spent the stop with **Pocket** up over the district instead of watching **Hedda Marchbank** work, which cost the crew their best set of eyes on the wards and bought them advance warning of what was waiting at the rift. **Lucien** watched from across the deck at disadvantage and came away certain only that he could not do it himself.
