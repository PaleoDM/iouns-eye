The licensed harvest station for widow crane tears, a hangar bolted into the Rift cliffs high above the **Inkwell** and dangling over the drop. Winches lower harvesters down the cliff face to the nesting plateaus. Run by **Oskra Deepwell**.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 12

Shut down when the crew arrived, and very quiet for a working place. The colony is shrinking, and the striking-rune trade that depends on it is about to feel it. The crew came in on a fake welfare inspection and went down the cliff with Oskra's harvesters, where three manticores and a chimera were waiting.
