## Description

A shady temple of **Glittergold** with an old distillery buried beneath it, run as a drophouse for contraband. **Garran** knows the place. The man who watches goods down there, **Zedediah**, takes a customary five gold to hold whatever you bring him, no questions asked.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 4

The crew stashed the Sorvellan broadsheets and the manifesto's linocut plate here after conning the printer. Zedediah took his five gold, then pressed Garran for a favor on the side: a good word with **Ralo** about sharing a pint sometime, now that Ralo had split from his wife.
