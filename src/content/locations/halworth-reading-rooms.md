## Description

A private bookshop and reading establishment in Rifthaven that serves noble families — the kind of place rich children get tutored. **La Marginalia** worked there as a contracted tutor.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 1

Named as the crew's first lead in tracing **La Marginalia**'s trail to the missing Sorvellan documents. Not yet visited as of Session 1.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 2

The crew came that same evening. The gnome proprietor **Sunflower** met them at the door, oblivious to the society of dead scholars in her attic. **Astrid** charmed the shop cat into revealing a hidden door and password to the third-floor attic, where the **ghost cabal** met. The crew also found four books with their ink drawn completely dry off the pages — a sign **Tabula Rasa** had been there first. In exchange for clearing out a loosed monarchist haunt, the cabal's **Lady Hypatra** gave up the next lead: La Marginalia's sister in **Gutterwick**.
