## Description

A private bookshop and reading establishment in Rifthaven that serves noble families — the kind of place rich children get tutored. **La Marginalia** worked there as a contracted tutor.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 1

Named as the crew's first lead in tracing **La Marginalia**'s trail to the missing Sorvellan documents. Not yet visited as of Session 1.
