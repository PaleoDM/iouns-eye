## Description

The airfield district, across the rift and high in the cliffs, above the altitude griffins care to fly. The airship docks here are substantial, and anything arriving passes a last checkpoint at the gates, where wards have to come down to keep from setting off the hangar alarms.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 8

The end of the convoy's night run. The *Meridian Queen* lay at dock with her sails furled and the sun coming up on the 20th of Pelor. The other two skiffs had come in ahead of the crew, both scorched. **Lord Ondorel** authenticated the **Evenbough** at the gate and it went down the gangplank into the hold.
