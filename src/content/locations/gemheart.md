## Description

The Gemheart District is one of Rifthaven's oldest districts and the heart of its artisan guilds. Jewelers, painters, metalsmiths and every other true masterwork guild do their business here. It is also the city's commercial hub, home to the Grand Souk. Gemheart is a strong union district, and the guilds keep outside business out.

The guilds elect their representatives during Glittergold's Holy Week. Voting runs all week, and the most influential people wait until the last day for the drama. The bankers hold a vote in the artisans' guild because, in the old days, everyone owed them money.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 16

The crew came down off the cliffs to **Kendrin**'s shop, where **Facette** told them her father had gone missing with the guild election closing that night. At the Guild Hall they met **Olo** of the Bankers Guild, **Soraya** of the Basket Weavers and **Briska** of the Farriers. When **Milo** dispelled **Jelfi**'s magic, she called on **Mammon**, and the crew fought her and three devils in the middle of the hall.
