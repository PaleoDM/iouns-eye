Cranes that nest on the Rift cliffs above Rifthaven. They stand as tall as a person and are the color of a gargoyle, with swollen red glands on either side of the beak. The "tears" come from those glands, and they go into every striking rune in the city.

When a chick hatches, the father feeds it on that magic instead of food, and then he dies. The hen mourns him for five years, and her tears are what the harvesters collect. They are wild and aggressive to anyone they do not recognize as kin. The harvesters at the **Widow Crane Rookery** use a ritual dance to get close, and never take more than a third.

An old Goliath folk song warns of the Widowmaker, a predator that wipes out whole colonies overnight. It comes when the widows are not allowed to mourn.

The flock migrates by following **The Widow** constellation.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 12

**Astrid** spoke with a hen through speak with animals, and **Pocket** vouched for her as his domestic human. The hen would not trust a human until **Milo** did the dance, which he did on a 37. Since then the hen has expected him back once a fortnight, reciting something dreadfully melancholy. A second crane, Shae, has her own designs on him.

The hens report chicks gone missing with no feathers and no blood, and widows not being allowed to mourn. Poachers come on a loud thing with spinning wings, carry sticks of fire, and take chicks soon after the fathers die. They also take tears in glass bottles of ink. The flock is split on whether to follow the Widow this season, and some have already gone.
