## Description

A Rift device sought by **Tabula Rasa**. The Mimeofoil does not merely forge a hand — it scrapes and rewrites the written record itself, lifting ink straight off a page and, lately, memory straight out of a living mind. Its mark is a wax seal bearing no sigil at all, a blank impression where an identity should be.

To forge convincingly it must be calibrated against one real, sealed original. The manifesto written in **Vendris Sorvellan**'s own hand is exactly the sample Tabula wants. A world where the device runs freely is a world where no signature, deed, or printed word can be trusted — which is precisely why **La Marginalia** tried, and failed, to move the crew with the stakes.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 3

Named at last. Its work had been visible all arc — **Sunflower**'s overwritten memory, the books drawn dry in the Halworth attic, the doppelganger wearing **Tomis Tidwell** — but in Maggie's bolt-hole the crew learned what it was for and what it needed. They also learned its damage can be undone: the blank seal recovered off the doppelganger, turned back on a victim, halted the ink bleeding out of **La Marginalia**'s wound. **Astrid** worked the cure.
