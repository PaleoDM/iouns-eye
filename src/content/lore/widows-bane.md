What drains from the glands of a widow crane after she has turned into a Widowmaker. It is not tears. It is far more volatile and more powerful, and maybe only the greatest archmages would know what to do with it. Making it is the real business of the poachers who steal the cranes' chicks.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 13

**Livid** gave **Astrid** the last two drops from the tips of her glands, in a small vial.
