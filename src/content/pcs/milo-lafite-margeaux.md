## Background

Nearly five centuries old. Originally a wealthy human merchant who was turned into a vampire and absolutely fell in love with the lifestyle. Rose to head of a vampire crime syndicate, conning marks with supernatural charm into bad business deals.

Then he got conned himself. Somehow tricked and cured of his vampirism against his will. His clan excommunicated him, mostly out of pity.

He still identifies as a vampire. He is desperate to return to that life.

## Character Notes

Extremely vain. A self-described looksmaxer. Down bad about the realization that nobody actually liked him — they liked the supernatural charisma. Struggling with mundane human experiences like walking in sunlight for the first time in centuries.
