## Background

Bringha was born to a wealthy human family with a long history of bardic tradition. She got kicked out of ballet school for fighting. From there she fell in with a street gang, and then in with an orc shaman woman who has become her master and sensei.

## Character Notes

Bringha is spunky and happy and quick to tell you exactly how she thinks things ought to be done. A bit of a hothead. She carries a dragon dance ribbon: a gift from her fun uncle.

She's young and a little immature, and she's been sent out into the world to grow up a little, see more than she's seen, and become more worldly.

## The Breach

Bringha spotted the movement inside the descent into the lair in Session 3, and the plan to draw it out with a flask of alchemist's fire was hers. The fire worked. The ambush did not: Rhydderch came out immediately, the rest of the party was still finding cover, and Bringha was left standing alone in front of the breach and surrounded.

She was dropped to zero in the first round, then rolled a natural twenty on her death save, came straight back up, put an ugly strike into the nearest zombie, and spent the first focus point of the campaign on a Step of the Wind to get clear.

## Culdir

Bringha came out of the Gloaming less clumsy, and she pays attention to the wind now. Turquoise lines run up her forearm. In Culdir she caught the lizard fryer's face going blank at the idea of Fill winning, and she stuck to the meat pie after that. Indri asked her to tie Chippy's shoelaces together, and she did.

At Edith's fire she got there first, chilled her fists, and sprayed cold water across one corner of the house until it stopped burning. The big fire spirit put her on the ground. Indri's lightning brought her back up, and she punched it back out into a magmin, which exploded all over her. Afterward she recognized Edith as one of the twins in the portrait over the mantel.
