## Background

A sailor and a scout from a coastal orc clan. Orcs of Astoria are typically matrilineal, and he found himself cast out of the clan by his now ex-wife. Finding life at home too awkward after this, Harsk became a wanderer. 

On the road he sometimes goes by **Polecat**, for how skinny and stealthy he can be.

## Open Threads

He left behind an ex-wife and a daughter, likely a preteen or young teen. He sends back what little money he can and feels real guilt about not being in her life.

He'll tell you the exile was harsh but not *exactly* unfair, and that he's largely made his peace with it. He is also clearly hiding parts of what actually happened.

## Rhydderch

Harsk's woodcraft failed him on the way north in Session 3. He read the ground badly, put the party into an uphill bushwhack, and led them onto an owlbear den before anyone understood what they were looking at. Falling back around it cost the better part of two hours and put them at the crash clearing in the dark.

At the breach he recognized the trapper whose fire he had shared once on the road. Rhydderch had been posted there, dead, with his own snares strung up around him. Harsk put the arrow through him that put him down, and gave him a farewell when the fighting was over.

## Culdir

Since the Gloaming, Harsk is in a better mood. The cold does not bother him anymore, and some nights he forgets anyone else needs a fire. His people call the storm titan Voshgaroth, the one who goes at the vanguard.

At Edith's fire a salamander told him a child of cold did not belong there, and he laughed at it. Edith threw herself into his arms afterward and told him he was so brave. He read her fear: not dying in a fire, but losing the pie contest next year.

## The Tree Line

Harsk rolled a natural 20 tracking from Fill's farm and found a shortcut along an old stone wall to the tree line. His Orcish warning shot came up a natural 1 and convinced Fill he was surrounded. He made up for it with an arrow through a bugbear's eye and another natural 20 that one-shot a magmin, along with a lot of piss jokes.
