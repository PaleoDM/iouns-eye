## Background

A Khoravar woman who grew up in a community built among ruins thick with residual magic. In places like that, people are often born with supernatural gifts. Indri's runs toward necromancy.

There's a practical reason that matters: with so much residual magic in the air, corpses don't fully rot on their own, and a body left to itself can rise. Someone has to help the dead decompose, a genuine community service, and Indri is one of the people who does it. She learned the work from her father.

## Open Threads

Her community was struck by a calamity, and her father is now bound to it full-time, running what amounts to endless triage. Indri is out wandering the ruins of old giant technology, hoping to find something that can solve the calamity and free her father from that duty. She has a real knack for salvaging from giant-made machines.
