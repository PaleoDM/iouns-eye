## Background

Officially assigned to protect a herd of displacer beasts from poachers during their planar migration to the Material Plane to breed. The migration is rare — once every sixteenth moon or so — leaving her with significant free time. (Exact cadence pending.)

## Day Jobs

**Official side job:** breeds chinchillas and toucans as messenger animals.

**Unofficial job:** demolition expert. Her brand of demolition involves sneaking in via Wild Shape and destroying things as an animal.

## Character Notes

A cryptid conspiracy theorist with a personal list of cryptids she desperately hopes to prove real.

Cool with basically all crimes — except poaching. Primarily takes jobs to keep herself busy for the fun of it.

Her animal messengers are critical to the crew's job logistics, which ties directly into the thieves' code of always communicating.
