## Background

A paladin with conviction in the power of the elements themselves. Laeryn does not belong to any church, order, or broader institution.

Laeryn believes the Titans have grown so active in the Triumvirate War because they were *awakened*, roused by an imbalance of some kind.

## Tenets

- Sow the seeds of creation amid the ashes of destruction.
- Lead with splendor and grace.
- Respect the elements, and fear their wrath.

He arrived at these through his own lived experience, not through any church or order.

## Character Notes

He frequently lies and claims there *is* an order behind him, because people adopt ideals more readily when they think someone larger already has.

## The Rope and the Message

Two things in Session 3 turned on Laeryn. Getting five people down a rope through a dead giant's cockpit is not a small problem, and he coordinated it as much by leadership as by hand, putting everyone down safe and in order.

Then he reached Eirlys with a message spell, the first contact anyone had had with her since she went into the Gloaming. What came back gave the party a clock and a way in: she would keep Marilissa talking for as long as she could hold her.

He landed the killing blow on Marilissa.

## The Reveal

At the fire after the dragon fell, Laeryn told the truth. Fifty years ago a man named Laeryn Vard made his name adventuring, then settled down and spent his life protecting a village he built in the woods. Dying, he asked a trusted friend to carry his name so the protection would outlive him. That friend kept the promise, and his son grew up watching him do it. The son wanted the name to do more than guard duty, so Laeryn Vard came out of retirement.

He is a changeling. His name is **Rasa**. His own face, when Bringha asked after it, kind of just goes away.

The party is keeping the secret for the village's sake. Bringha welcomed not-Laeryn to the table without missing a beat. Harsk said he needed to think on it. Yu had felt the Everlight through this man, and the real Laeryn had been hers.

## The Bait and the Blast

Killing Sunset-Is-Nigh took a target, and Laeryn made himself one. He spent the whole shit-talking preamble drifting into the harpoon's target square, missed with a javelin to hold the dragon's attention, and stood his ground while it charged. The splash from the shot hit him for exactly enough to kill him outright. The Afanc's boon flared on its own and bought him the hit point between unconscious and dead.

## Culdir

On the road Rasa wore a new face most days, with a glow under the skin, and kept to Laeryn's face around strangers. In Culdir an apple seller told him Fill had come to town a ruffian until an adventurer put him in his place. Fill said one of the adventurers at the festival had saved his life, and Edith credits "that Laeryn" with setting him straight.

At Edith's fire Rasa told her to run when Bringha dropped. He brought his maul down on the big fire spirit, and the earth rumbled and snuffed it out before his smite could take. Afterward he used his brand to see what kept Edith's fire lit, and her cane flickered into a candy cane.

## Fill

Rasa's javelin went through a bugbear with a smite behind it, and the earth came up through its legs and held it. Fill followed the javelin back to its thrower and knew Laeryn. Rasa knocked one bugbear out alive and did not spare the armored one: the earth broke its grip on Fill, his maul broke its neck, and the earth held the body up afterward. Fill says he owes Laeryn again. Rasa made him promise to answer for Briochebane's actions.
