## Background

Calm, very well-dressed, mild-mannered. Doesn't raise his voice.

Originally a white-collar fixer for criminals — forging documents, producing blackmail material, the kind of work that lives between the bookkeeper and the blackmailer.

While fixing a set of genealogy documents for a client family, he opened an ancient scroll in a catacomb belonging to that family and was struck by a surge of wild magic. (Family name pending.)

## Character Notes

His magic is **not Fey, not elemental** — it is procedural and contractual in nature. He uses it the way he used to use his legal proclivities.

Resents being treated as a tool. Preferred being a "person for hire."

## Open Threads

His first job after gaining his powers was working for the **Ashen Barristers** — a private legal society of lawyers, administrators, notaries, and clerks — in their magical writ-tampering unit. They might still help him in a bind if he were to call on them.
