## Background

Mid-level goon in the da Pinci crime syndicate.

Quick to give people nicknames they hate. Jovial. Happy with his mid-level status, with no ambition to climb the ranks. Secretly has a big heart, but absolutely does shady, unscrupulous things.

## Family

Has a son, **RJ** (Ralo Junior). In a trial separation from RJ's mother, and it is going badly. His entire family resents him for not climbing the ladder, and everyone is after a bigger piece of his pie. He resents them right back.

His safe house is his elderly mother's place. She gives him constant grief, but it is the only refuge he has now that he is separated from RJ's mother.

## Open Threads

His previous boss, Lonnie "Lobster" da Pinci, was recently killed. Significant pressure has fallen on Ralo to step up and climb the ranks in the syndicate — pressure he does not want.
