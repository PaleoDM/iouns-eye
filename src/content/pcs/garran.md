## Background

Freelance mercenary. Primary reputation: gets the job done. Says very little about his past.

Surly and silent. Treats business as business. A big believer in doing work, and doing it well.

A classic tough guy who is not a thug and not clumsy. Dexterous, acrobatic, smart. Doesn't suffer fools. Has no aspiration to climb ranks.

## Character Notes

Holds the thieves' core lesson dear: stick to the plan, always communicate. He sees it as the glue that holds crews together.

## Open Threads

Walked away from a job because the employer was dishonest about the terms. Ambiguous whether he walked for morals or for the principle of having been misled. Either way, there is a bounty on his head for the unfinished work.
