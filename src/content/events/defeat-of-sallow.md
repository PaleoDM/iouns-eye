## What Happened

The party stormed Sallow's command center inside the rooted juggernaut. The fight opened with Vicas charging straight in on nearly empty focus points, declaring he would race Sallow to the end. Lyra came in close behind with Dawn's Radiance; Thump landed Grasping Vine; Rooker dropped Evard's Black Tentacles. Sallow answered with a powerful flame burst that caught Lyra, Thump, and Rooker on fire, forcing them to spend a full round on damage control while Vicas and Azrael kept hitting.

In round three, Sallow loosed a Chain Lightning. Rooker reached for a counterspell; Sallow burned a legendary save and let it fly, dropping Lyra, Thump, and Rooker on top of an already-downed Azrael. Vicas was the last one standing with no focus left to heal. He sprinted to Lyra, poured a healing potion down her throat, and got her back up. Lyra's Mass Healing Word and Thump's follow-up healing flipped the fight in a single round.

Vicas finished Sallow personally, driving his spear up through Sallow's chin and out the other side, then shoving the body through a gap in the juggernaut's chassis. Sallow fell to the foot of the trapped juggernaut and was impaled vertically on the same caltrop-pike Hrothgar had used to root the thing in place.

## Consequences

Sallow is dead. Thump reclaimed the seed of the Myreshade from the heart of the chamber, accepting the duty to replant it in secret. The Turquoise Timber Trust's military arm is broken, and the assault on Ishetar ends here. The campaign closes on the heroes' epilogues.
