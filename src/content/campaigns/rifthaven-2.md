Rifthaven: Knaves and Knights picks up roughly one year after the events of Rifthaven: Root of Rebellion. The campaign opens on 22 Ioun 1130 TA, in the King Tides window of high summer.

The campaign reframes the city through a new lens: the upper class as viewed by criminals, rather than the lower class as viewed by heroes. The party are professional scoundrels — fixers, specialists, and unseen hands. Their targets are nobles, merchants, politicians, and institutions, not monsters or Rift abominations. Failure looks like jail, debt, exile, or a knife in an alley.

The crew works in a Rifthaven still shaped by the previous campaign's revolution. The do-gooder heroes of the Sparkworks District — the knights of this world's preferred story — are a hazard to be navigated, not a faction to ally with. Pressure on the underworld is shifting: the da Pinci crime syndicate is suddenly unstable following the death of Lonnie "Lobster" da Pinci, and a rival crew funded by an unknown wealthy patron is rising fast. A piece of Rift-pulled magitech that forges handwriting perfectly is the talk of the criminal underbelly.

The party operates by the thieves' unwritten rule of the city: **stick to the plan, always communicate.**
