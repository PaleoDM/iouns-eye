**Hearth and Hatchlight** is a Second Age prequel campaign set during the long stalemate of the Triumvirate War — the centuries-long conflict between Dragons, Giants, and Titans. The party are small folk caught up in a hidden plan to bring something new into the world: a ritual that has not been performed since before the war began. Day-to-day life is small and warm; the war is everywhere and nowhere, in the price of bread and the patches of scorched earth between villages.

The campaign is in early planning. Pitch and Session Zero materials are being assembled.
