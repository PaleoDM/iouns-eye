Ishetar 2.0 picks up fifty-four years after the original Ishetar campaign. The colony has grown from a fledgling outpost to a thriving settlement on the verge of independence from the Aroiin Empire. Tensions with the empire are at an all-time high, and the frontier of Ekkorai remains dangerously wild. The campaign follows five new heroes as they defend Ishetar from converging threats: the Turquoise Timber Trust's exploitative logging operation, an undead army mobilizing from the sea, the Celestial Seed cult hunting an Aasimar infant, and the slow fracturing of the Aroiin Empire itself.

The campaign concluded in Session 34 with the defeat of Sallow inside the trapped juggernaut at the gates of Ishetar, followed by epilogues for each of the five heroes. Thump returned the Myreshade seed to the heart of the forest and stewarded its regrowth. Lyra took to the road on Lady Rowena's mandate to unite the people of Ekkorai. Rooker hung up his adventuring hat and helped found an arcane school under Headmistress Sombra. Azrael and Th0l (with Vicas, for a time) set their sights on bringing down the Temple of the Celestial Seed. Vicas, years later and at the end of his life, returned to Ishetar to be laid beneath the maple tree at the grave Thump had prepared, and to join Shallah.
