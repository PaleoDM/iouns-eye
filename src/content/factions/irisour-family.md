A noble family of Rifthaven and one of the biggest buyers of widow crane tears at the **Widow Crane Rookery**.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 12

A month before the session, a young Irisour son paid **Oskra Deepwell** a sack of coin to take a lady he was courting on a night tour of the nests. The cranes remember the pair in fancy plumage leading strangers to the cliffs, strangers with glass bottles of ink who took tears in them. The strangers have come back on their own ever since, stealing chicks.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 13

**Olivier** wants to break a story tying the **Circle of the Broken Tusk** to the Irisours. Nothing at the den proved it either way.
