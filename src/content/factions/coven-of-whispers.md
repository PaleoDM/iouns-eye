A quiet network that carries messages witches do not trust to ordinary post. Dr. Riddenour used it to answer Eirlys about the cold lights, folding her letter into a parcel of ankheg milk that traveled hand to hand.

Almost nothing else is known. Eirlys says the Coven itself is something altogether else, and that women like her call themselves sisters *to* it rather than members *of* it. The sisters are kept quiet.
