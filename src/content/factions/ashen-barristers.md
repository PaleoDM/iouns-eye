A private legal society of lawyers, administrators, notaries, and clerks operating out of Rifthaven. Their reputation is unremarkable; their magical wing is not.

The Ashen Barristers run a **magical writ-tampering unit** — discreet specialists in forging, altering, and contesting magical documents and binding texts.

## Connection to the Party

**Lucien** worked his first post-awakening job in the writ-tampering unit. The Barristers might still extend a favor to him in a bind, depending on the circumstances.
