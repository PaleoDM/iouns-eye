A long-standing guild of monster hunters. Vampires, werewolves, and whatever else they decide qualifies. Their mark is a numeral three with each stroke made out of a stake, worn on the inside of the wrist.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 9

One of theirs came aboard the *Meridian Queen* at the wedding brunch, armored the way half the guests were armored, and made a play on **Dolphus Garner** with a silvered pistol. **Garran** got a hand on her before she could close and dragged her back out of range. She told him the groom was a monster and that he had to let her go.

**Ottavia Brann** recognized the tattoo and chose to keep it quiet rather than let the room work out who the target had been.

**Milo**, who has his own reasons to care, spent the walk home wondering who else at that brunch the Third Act had been watching.
