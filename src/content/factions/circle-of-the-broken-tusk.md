An extremist fringe druid circle. Most of Rifthaven would call them terrorists. Their goals are usually pro-nature, and they will make very uncomfortable bedfellows to reach them. Their Druidic has a distinct accent, and **Astrid** can recognize their hand on a wall.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 13

The crew found their den in a cave on the Rift cliffs, guarded by a collared cloaker with Druidic instructions for getting past it. Inside, they had chained a transformed widow crane, **Livid**, and drained her glands for **Widow's Bane**. They are the poachers the cranes described, and they make these monsters on purpose. Astrid decided they were hardly druids at all.

Five of them came back to the den on an air skiff, among them a dragonborn, a lizardfolk, an aarakocra, and one who was on fire. Livid ate them all. Judging by the den, there are a dozen or more left, nesting three quarries over and down a hidden ravine. **Olivier** suspects a tie to the **Irisour Family**.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 14

Their camp is built across ledges on the cliff, a couple of quarries over from the den. Their matron holds court in a cavern on one of the upper layers, above a distillery and an inner sanctum. The crew crashed the circle's own skiff into the lowest ledge and killed everyone on it, lizardfolk mages and azer among them. A druid on the next ledge up told **Astrid** they had no known enemies, and argued that the cranes are a resource like fruit from a tree. That ledge holds the pens, a ritual pool, a druid with a hawk familiar, and two kept owlbears, one of them enormous.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 15

At the distillery its people were split. One faction had orders to get the **Widow's Bane** out at all costs and had no use for **Mother Lycricia**. The other waited on her order to fall back. The crew killed the last of the pens ledge, both owlbears included. Mother Lycricia then came to the crew herself. Her partners had double-crossed her, and she pulled the Circle back rather than lose more of its number. She left the **Irisour Family**'s halflings and the **Tabula Rasa** agents to hold the inner sanctum alone.
