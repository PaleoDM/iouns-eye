An extremist fringe druid circle. Most of Rifthaven would call them terrorists. Their goals are usually pro-nature, and they will make very uncomfortable bedfellows to reach them. Their Druidic has a distinct accent, and **Astrid** can recognize their hand on a wall.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 13

The crew found their den in a cave on the Rift cliffs, guarded by a collared cloaker with Druidic instructions for getting past it. Inside, they had chained a transformed widow crane, **Livid**, and drained her glands for **Widow's Bane**. They are the poachers the cranes described, and they make these monsters on purpose. Astrid decided they were hardly druids at all.

Five of them came back to the den on an air skiff, among them a dragonborn, a lizardfolk, an aarakocra, and one who was on fire. Livid ate them all. Judging by the den, there are a dozen or more left, nesting three quarries over and down a hidden ravine. **Olivier** suspects a tie to the **Irisour Family**.
