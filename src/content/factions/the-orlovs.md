A rival noble family with a deep hatred for **House Ondorel**.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 6

**Ralo** turned up moon runes in the **Silver Serpent**'s black book showing that the Serpent had already sourced a buyer for the Ondorel instrument before ever running the job. The Orlovs would almost certainly pay for it just to flaunt it in the Ondorels' faces, which answered **Lucien**'s objection that an instrument with a reputation like that would be impossible to move. The fencing was handled down the line before the crew ever picked up the score.
