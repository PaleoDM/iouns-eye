A Rifthaven crime family with a crustacean naming convention. The syndicate operates by **squeezing locals and businesses just the right amount** — never enough to provoke a serious response, just enough to keep the city's inertia working in their favor.

## Recent Upheaval

**Lonnie "Lobster" da Pinci** — a mid-tier boss within the syndicate — was recently killed. The cause traces back to a throwaway protection arrangement with the Aletheia Procedure that he had been quietly paying off; when those payments lapsed, his rivals saw their opening and moved on him. His death has destabilized his stretch of the operation and put pressure on his underlings — most notably **Ralo Battaglia** — to climb the ladder, fill the gap, or be filled in the gap.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 6

The story behind Lonnie's death changed. **Ralo** ran into **Renzo "Basta" Corvi** at an Ondorel gala and named him as Lonnie's killer, then supplied the part nobody had: Lonnie had been sleeping with Corvi's wife. What the syndicate had read as a crime between two families was a crime of passion, and it has complicated the relationship between those families ever since.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 7

The story changed again. **Stella Corvi** told **Ralo** that she was never sleeping with Lonnie. She is **Gatito** blood, she was working him for her family, and the two syndicates were close to a brokered truce with Lonnie as the way in. Corvi killed the operation along with the man.

Stella now wants Ralo to broker that truce himself, and she is running out of time to produce something for her family. She warned him that within days she will either be cut loose, costing the crew an ally, or shown mercy, making the crew an enemy of the Gatitos.

Ralo was also present when the crew killed four Gatitos at the **Lamplighter Market**. The families' uneasy peace has held since Lonnie died. A da Pinci was there for that, and the Gatitos will learn it.

## Notable Members

- **Lonnie "Lobster" da Pinci** — deceased mid-tier boss; killed shortly before the events of *Knaves and Knights*
- **Ralo Battaglia** — current mid-level goon, under pressure to step up
