An old-money noble house of Rifthaven, silver dragonborn, carrying the rot of generations of unchecked entitlement. Led by the patriarch **Kelvaryn Sorvellan**, with his son and heir **Vendris** set to step into the family's hereditary council seat.

The house's first appearance drives the arc **The Manifesto**: a set of politically damaging documents written by Vendris in his youth, bearing his own seal, was taken by the woman known as **La Marginalia**. The Sorvellans hired the crew at the **Brimstone Bath** to recover them before they reach the broadsheet printers — 200 gold a head, no advance.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 3

The house's reach into **Gutterwick** showed plainly: rent-men shadowed the crew to the **Almshouse** but would not enter the protected ground. The job's price tightened, too. With the manifesto's linocut already at the printer, only half the pay sits in hand — the rest depends on stopping it at the press. And the document the Sorvellans want destroyed is the same one **Tabula Rasa** needs to calibrate the **Mimeofoil**, putting the family's quiet scandal at the center of a far larger game.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 4

The job closed, and the house's rot showed all the way down. **Kelvaryn** sent **Vendris** and hired goons to settle up, with orders to murder the crew rather than pay in full. The house is broke behind the marble: only half the promised coin ever came. Vendris covered part of the rest himself with the deed to an **Inkwell** shophouse, his own inheritance, and still owes the crew a hundred gold. The Sorvellans got the manifesto neutralized, but they made enemies of the scoundrels who saved the heir's life.
