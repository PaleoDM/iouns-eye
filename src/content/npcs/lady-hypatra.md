A ghost of the dead scholars' cabal that meets in the hidden attic of the **Halworth Reading Rooms**. A martyr in life, murdered for her knowledge. A close friend of **La Marginalia**.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 2

Spoke for the cabal in striking a deal with the crew: clear out the loosed poltergeists, and the ghosts would give up a lead on La Marginalia. When the haunt was cleared, she made good, pointing the crew to Maggie's sister **Wrenna** down in the **Gutterwick** district.
