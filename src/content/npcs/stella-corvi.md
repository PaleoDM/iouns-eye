Wife of **Renzo "Basta" Corvi**, and **Gatito** by blood. Born Stella Gatito, third daughter of a second aunt. Not important, by her own account, but important enough that the lifestyle comes with obligations to the family.

She keeps rooms above a nightclub out on one of the decks that sit over the rift, a place that stopped being fashionable about fifteen years ago. She holds court there and hosts semi-important people, and the bar downstairs screens her visitors.

A clouded leopard tabaxi. She wears the glamour of a blonde human woman in pearls, and the pearls are the glamour.

She and **Ralo Battaglia** have history. She calls him "Batty G."

## Knaves and Knights, Session 7

The crew came to her looking for her husband's whereabouts, and she made them work for it.

She took off her pearls and dropped the disguise herself, and told **Ralo** the truth about **Lonnie "Lobster" da Pinci**. She was never sleeping with him. She was working him. The **Gatitos** and the **da Pincis** were close to a truce and Lonnie was the way in. Renzo did something emotional, killed the man, and killed the peace deal along with him.

**Lucien** had subtle cast Detect Thoughts before any of that, so she never knew a spell went off in her house. On the surface she was doing arithmetic: what does Ralo want, and how much can she squeeze him for it. Pushed deeper, she gave up more. She has already made peace with the fact that her husband is going to die. She knew about the lute job and had tried to talk him out of it. And underneath all of it, the thing she actually wants: Ralo has to broker the peace himself. Nothing less will do.

She felt the probe and called it. When the room got sharp, Lucien put it to her plainly, and she folded and gave up the **Lamplighter Market**.

**Astrid** lifted the pearl necklace off the serving tray on the way out. It is a Necklace of Disguise. Stella does not yet know it is gone.

## The Warning

Ralo hung back, and in the hallway she was a different woman. She told him how the week goes. In a couple of days the family comes to see how she has been doing for them. Renzo will probably be dead by then, and she will throw him under the bus and pin every bit of it on him to save herself. After that it goes one of two ways. They cut her loose, and the crew has lost a powerful ally. Or they show her mercy, and the crew has made an enemy of the **Gatitos**. She does not think either one is good for them.

She needs the truce fast, and she said why. There is a new player in town, and she assumed the crew had heard about **La Marginalia** and the **Tabula Rasa**.

Then she asked after **RJ**, who she calls Batty Junior, and told Ralo she would tell **DB** he was still thinking about her.
