A unicorn of Mount Celestia, with a mane that runs from blue to lilac, eyes that blaze white, and hooves that never touch the ground. It calls itself the voice of compassion.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 18

**Lucien**'s magic surged in **Severin Mortmain**'s parlor, and Stardust stepped out of a split in reality. It crushed the leech riding **Tusk** under its hooves and greeted Lucien in Celestial as Caretaker. It wrapped the crew in a mile of silence and flew their cart home to the shop in **Inkwell**. It warned them about the foul creatures their host deals with, and told them to take great care in his cellars. Twice in that battle, it said, victory had come from the power of friendship. **Ralo** gave it an apple, and **Milo** got a hug from wings it should not have had. It was **Astrid**'s first unicorn. It could not promise to come again.
