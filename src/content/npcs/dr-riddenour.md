A researcher with an estate south of Aberblaidd, known so far only through a letter. Eirlys calls her a wonderful person and a brilliant researcher, and had written to her about the one cold light she had seen above the Gloaming.

The reply came folded into a parcel of ankheg milk, carried quietly by the Coven of Whispers because she did not trust it to ordinary post. It named the light. Hatchlight, they are calling it. Some of the exposed manifest strange powers. Others blight, black veiny patterns climbing the arm and into the shoulder. She is gathering the exposed at her estate to study what comes of it, and asked that anyone touched by the light be sent her way.

Eirlys wrote to her about one light. The party has now seen three, and every one of them is marked. They have an invitation.
