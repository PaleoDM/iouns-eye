The incumbent head of the Bankers Guild in **Gemheart**. A favor from him costs a little grease. In one bricklayer's experience, he always says thank you.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 16

**Lucien** spotted the gaudy net of caged gems in his beard, and **Astrid**'s Detect Magic found it ringing with enchantment. When **Milo** dispelled **Jelfi**, the gems in his beard cracked and he looked woozy. As the fight broke out, **Garran** watched him turn into a tall thing wrapped in chains. It scared **Ralo**, and Ralo scared it right back. Garran killed it.
