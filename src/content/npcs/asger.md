Edith Applegarth's twin brother. The two of them appear as five-year-olds in the faintly magic portrait over Edith's mantel. Edith named him when Indri asked about it.

Fill had always heard that Edith's brother died in one of the last wars, and very few people in Culdir are old enough to remember him.

## Hearth and Hatchlight, Session 6

At the tree line, an armored bugbear told Fill that its master wants Briochebane, and that they would take good care of him the same way they take care of Asger. He would live forever. The master always takes care of his things.
