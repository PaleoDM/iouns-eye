**Zedediah**'s whistle-whisker, a naked-mole-rat-like creature with a long, anteater-style snout and a set of big whiskers, riding along in his hood. Whistle-whiskers aren't normally domesticated, and Zedediah's the reason anyone in the crew knows they exist at all. Communicates in little R2-D2 style beeps and whistles. Has one of the best noses in the tunnels, good enough to tell a genuine keg of family whiskey from a decoy, but it has to get drunk first to do its job.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 10

Rode along with **Zedediah** down into the **Ironclad District** to sniff out the real whiskey kegs from the decoys his family laid out to fool looters. Spent most of the trip perched and distracted, tugging at Zed's ear whenever something down in the tunnels caught its attention.
