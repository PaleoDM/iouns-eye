A strange old man who has been kind to each of our heroes, in turn if not all at once. Many quietly wonder whether he's even human.

He turns up right when he's needed, and he always has exactly the thing. Out of torches, and here's a man selling torches. Out of rope, and he's selling rope. Cold and tired, and there's his warm fire. Always convenient. Always a little too convenient.
