A green dragon wyrmling, only weeks old, living somewhere below the lair of Death at Sunset. He is hers. She broke off the fight that killed her because she was protecting a clutch of eggs, and he is what came of that choice.

He has not been seen. The party learned of him from Eirlys after they freed her, and everything known about him so far comes from her five days at his bench.

He called Marilissa and made her his, and he has had Marilissa and Eirlys working on a single problem: a venom that gets through resistance and immunity. He wants a weapon against the things his own blood cannot touch.

He is directly beneath a loaded Ancient Fire Giant Harpoon of Dragonslaying, still aimed down into the wound in his mother's lair, and he does not know it.

## Hearth and Hatchlight, Session 4

The harpoon is what killed him. Bringha drew him out with shit-talk and dragon jokes, and he liked her enough to offer her Marilissa's old job. Laeryn baited him into the target square, and Indri, meshed with the ship's computer, put the harpoon through the top of his skull and stapled it into the creek below. Then the ship itself came down on him, the same hull that killed his mother.

What the lair told them afterward: he was about twenty years old, not weeks, and he had never once been outside. His mother whispered his name into his head before she died, one name for a whole clutch, and he ate his siblings to be the only one left to claim it. She impressed a fear of the harpoon on him, and that fear kept him underground his whole life. He kept a shrine to her around one of her teeth.

His hoard held a thousand gold, a potion of fire breath, a dust of disappearance he never worked out, and two giant-cut striking runes on the shrine. The party took the tooth, the scales, and the bones.
