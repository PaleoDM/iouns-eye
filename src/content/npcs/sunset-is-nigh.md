A green dragon wyrmling, only weeks old, living somewhere below the lair of Death at Sunset. He is hers. She broke off the fight that killed her because she was protecting a clutch of eggs, and he is what came of that choice.

He has not been seen. The party learned of him from Eirlys after they freed her, and everything known about him so far comes from her five days at his bench.

He called Marilissa and made her his, and he has had Marilissa and Eirlys working on a single problem: a venom that gets through resistance and immunity. He wants a weapon against the things his own blood cannot touch.

He is directly beneath a loaded Ancient Fire Giant Harpoon of Dragonslaying, still aimed down into the wound in his mother's lair, and he does not know it.
