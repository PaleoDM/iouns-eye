**Astrid**'s familiar. A nightjar.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 7

Worked every single round of the fight at the **Lamplighter Market**, flying in to help whoever on the crew needed it most.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 8

Took a smaller shape than usual for the convoy job, small enough to sit in a pocket and pass for something ordinary. At the **Coronet Gate** he flew a circuit over the district and spotted messenger birds working back and forth near the gondola stations, which is not something that happens over the rift by accident. That warning is the only reason the crew saw the griffins coming.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 9

Pointed at **Hedda Marchbank**, who is terrified of birds, and got her off the crew's back in the corridor outside the security vault. No spell, no roll worth the name. Just a nightjar, aimed.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 12

Vouched for **Astrid** to a suspicious widow crane hen as his pet, a domestic human. The hen was pleased to see some humans learning their place. When a second crane made a play for **Milo**, Pocket told the ladies this one was still being domesticated.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 13

Kept **Livid** company while **Astrid**'s speak with animals came up, assuring her that Astrid was almost done because it takes her a while to relearn.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 14

Scouted the **Circle of the Broken Tusk**'s pens for **Astrid**, who watched through his eyes. A druid's hawk came out of the sky at him, and Astrid called him home.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 15

Scouted the tunnels inside the cliff for **Astrid**, up a chute to the pens and down the fork to the distillery. Perched under the distillery floor, he heard two factions of the **Circle of the Broken Tusk** arguing over **Mother Lycricia**, and the cheep of a chick.
