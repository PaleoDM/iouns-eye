The matron of the **Circle of the Broken Tusk**. Her people wait on her word. She takes the shape of a celestial warthog with pegasus wings and two gnarled tusks, one of them broken off.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 15

She came down over the quarry wall in her warthog shape and asked the crew whether it had to be blood. When **Astrid** called it poaching, she said poaching is when you kill the animals. To her the Widowmaker transformation is nature's immune system against a heinous capitalist act of husbandry, and turning a widow or two would shut the farming down.

**Lucien** read her best. She is profiting off all of it, and her partners, the **Irisour Family** and **Tabula Rasa**, had just double-crossed her. She asked the crew's price, and **Ralo** held out for an executive producer credit. So she pulled the Circle back, left the Irisours and the Tabula agents to fight to the death, and told the crew where to find the hen and the chicks. Then she flew back up the cliffs.
