A half-giant warlord who holds territory in the region with a claim of thugs at his back. He's hard to move against, and nobody's sure whether he's tied to the real giant communities, so taking him down risks waking something far worse.

Rumor has it that if you play a certain flute, he might let you pass through his territory. Otherwise he's a hard-ass.
