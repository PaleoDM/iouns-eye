A gnome gem cutter, not much older than **Facette**, who ran against **Kendrin** in the guild election. She got far too good, far too fast, without the proper training.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 16

Her campaign posters said Kendrin would abandon you like he had abandoned the race. By the last day of voting, the heads of the bankers, the bakers and the basket weavers were all wearing her rings. She worked the Guild Hall in a tiara of rubies, sapphires and emeralds. **Astrid**'s Detect Magic found her enhanced, not disguised.

She flirted with **Milo**, and he dispelled her. The tiara cracked, she got older and grungier, and she began to smell of rotten eggs and caught fire. She called on **Mammon**. Two devils came, and **Olo** turned into a third. **Lucien** dropped her, but the broken tiara grew legs and tore her head off, and her body got up without it. She fought on with a scimitar made of shadow until **Ralo** cut her down.

She had made a deal with a devil. It made her gems supernaturally good, and it let her sway whoever she gave them to. On election night she meant to crystallize Kendrin's heart and eat it. The crew found him alive in her cellar, and Ralo kept her scimitar.
