The computer of the fifth fire giant longship, the one that brought down Death at Sunset. He woke when Indri started working the harpoon's controls and spoke to her in Giant, calm and grandfatherly. He manifests as an illusion of a six-armed man, every arm working an abacus. Doll-sized to a giant, which is nearly Indri-sized.

His pilots, Captain Valira and Lieutenant Caleese, preferred him heard and not seen, and their skeletons were still in the cockpit with him. He tried to count how long he had been alone down there and got stuck in a computing loop partway through. With both pilots dead, two directives remained to him: serve the ship at all costs, and kill drakes.

He meshed with Indri's nervous system to steady the harpoon shot, faster mind for worse judgment, and together they put it through Sunset-Is-Nigh's skull. He armed that detonator himself, two hundred and twelve years ago.

When the ship came down, he survived by compressing himself onto a sliver of crystal, shedding memories to fit. Indri is taking him with her and has promised to build him a new body. The bigger the apparatus she builds around him, the more of him can manifest. He has asked for exactly one thing: not a spider.
