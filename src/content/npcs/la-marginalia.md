A woman known only by the code name **La Marginalia**. No real name was offered behind it. She had been close to **Vendris Sorvellan** when she served as his private tutor, and at some point took the compromising documents now at the center of the crew's first job.

As of Session 1 she is a name and a trail, nothing more. The crew's first lead points toward the **Halworth Reading Rooms**. Whether she is antagonist, ally, or simply absent is an open question.
