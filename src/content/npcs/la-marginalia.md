A woman known only by the code name **La Marginalia**. No real name was offered behind it. She had been close to **Vendris Sorvellan** when she served as his private tutor, and at some point took the compromising documents now at the center of the crew's first job. To her sister and her friends she is known as "Maggie."

As of Session 1 she is a name and a trail, nothing more. The crew's first lead points toward the **Halworth Reading Rooms**. Whether she is antagonist, ally, or simply absent is an open question.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 2

A clearer shape emerged. She was a close friend of the dead scholars' cabal in the Halworth attic, who revere her, and she has a living sister, **Wrenna Tidwell**, down in **Gutterwick**. Two weeks back she was wounded and went to ground in a safe house in the district. Her brother-in-law **Tomis** had begun pressing Wrenna to give her up, and the crew may not be the only ones hunting her — **Tabula Rasa** appears to be closing on her too.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 3

Met at last, in the flesh, in a bolt-hole beneath the **Almshouse** in Gutterwick. She was wounded, her room buried under conspiracy writing, the work of a woman trying to get herself down on paper before she was gone. Her "wound" was the proof: she bled ink, not blood, the same unwriting that took **Sunflower**. **Tabula Rasa** had begun erasing her two weeks back.

The conversation was a careful dance, complicated by the favor she owed the crew for saving Wrenna. She had the manifesto, written in **Vendris**'s own hand, and the soft spot she carries for the younger Sorvellan is matched by open contempt for the elder over what his name does to Gutterwick. She tried to appeal to the crew's sense of heroism — the **Mimeofoil** would end trust in any signed or printed word — but they are scoundrels, and that did not land. What landed was the catch: the manifesto's linocut had already gone to the printer, so half their pay rode on stopping it at the press.

**Astrid**, reasoning it out with **Milo**, used the blank Tabula seal taken off the doppelganger to heal Maggie's ink-wound. It worked. Restored and far chipper, Maggie made good — she gave up the manifesto and the printer's location. Neither ally nor enemy yet, but no longer just a name on a trail.
