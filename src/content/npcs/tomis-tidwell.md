Husband of **Wrenna Tidwell** in **Gutterwick**. Once, by his wife's account, a good man who simply wanted to keep his head down and stay clear of her sister **La Marginalia**.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 2

Lately Tomis had turned, growing obsessed with Maggie and pressing Wrenna to give her up for a rent collector's bribe. He came home to find the crew in his house and his wife on her way out, and he raised a shillelagh, demanding they all go turn Maggie in at once. Then the crew watched his face morph and rearrange in real time, his voice pitching down to match — not Tomis at all, but something wearing his shape. **Ralo** instinctively suspected a trick. Where the real Tomis is, and what has taken his face, remains open.
