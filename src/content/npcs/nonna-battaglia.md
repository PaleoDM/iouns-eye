**Ralo Battaglia**'s elderly mother, whose apartment doubles as his safe house. She wants a new tennis bracelet and a son who provides better, and she catalogues his failings without breaking stride — his weight, his estranged wife, his refusal to climb the ladder.

Behind Ralo's back she has been soliciting lawyers on his behalf over his separation, which is how the matter reached **Sage** and, through her, **Lucien**. She begged Ralo to go meet the lawyer at the **Brimstone Bath**, and he reluctantly went.
