A neighbor in the **Inkwell** district. Olivier knocked on the crew's door at the very end of Session 5 carrying a crystal the size of a ball, come to introduce the new owners to something the locals call the Aether-waves, the way Inkwell's denizens stay current on the news.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 6

Olivier arrived at the new house with the aether crystal and made the introduction. He turned out to have an interest in cryptids, and seemed especially interested in **Astrid**'s parrots, asking whether they were the mysterious "Passenger Parrots" and whether those were truly extinct. Astrid was not much interested in him or his cryptids.

The news he carried mattered more than the small talk. The city is abuzz that the *Meridian Queen* is returning to Rifthaven, and that she will be in port a fortnight before departing on another circuit of Astoria. That detail set the crew's clock for the Songbird job.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 12

Showed up at the crew's door with a cracked crystal and footage from the Rift cliffs: widow cranes asleep on their nests, then something huge and gangly crossing the dark behind them, then a scream. He ran and could not make himself stop, and his crystal man **Kalish** has not been seen since. He wanted to know what was killing the cranes, and his show was about to lose its slot to an infomercial without a scoop.

He could not pay. He came to the crew because the neighborhood calls them a mystery squad, and is very clear that they are not heroes. **Astrid** took the job in exchange for a weekly guest spot on his show, where she brings on animals and he holds them. He sang them the old Goliath song of the Widowmaker, learned by ear in a language he does not speak.

**Oskra Deepwell** called him a narc, and Astrid lifted his recording crystal so the foreman would talk. He followed the crew down the cliff anyway, and healed **Lucien** after the manticores.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 13

Warned the crew that the creature in the den was probably muzzled for a reason, then got **Kalish** out of his cage. The job was technically done, but he came along anyway, to heal and not to kill. He asked for his crystal back to film the poachers, and **Astrid** refused to leave any record. He pitched it as investigative journalism instead, the **Circle of the Broken Tusk** tied to the **Irisour Family**, with masks for uniforms. Then he agreed to hang back sixty feet.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 14

Rode into the poachers' camp in a chest with **Kalish**, then climbed out to help **Astrid** heal the crew. He coached **Lucien** on a goat-bleat illusion as the resident bird expert, and it lured the camp's owlbears down.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 15

Spent his last two cure wounds on **Ralo** and **Milo** after the owlbears. Then he went with **Kalish** and **Livid** to the distillery after Livid's chicks.
