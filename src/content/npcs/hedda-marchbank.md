A dwarven woman with tight orange curls braided down both sides of her face, hired by the hour. She is a contracted master abjurer, which means she puts wards on other people's cargo and takes them off again at every checkpoint. She does not do divination and does not want to. She is terrified of flying, does not care for birds, and spends any airborne stretch belayed to whatever she is riding.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 8

She warded the case that ran from **House Ondorel** to the hold of the *Meridian Queen*, and piloted the crew's skiff besides. She did not know which of the three cases held the real **Evenbough** and made a point of saying she did not want to.

She named her three danger points up front. The customs house at the **Coronet Gate**, the rift crossing where a skiff has to be towed by gondola, and the hangar at **Witherwind**. At each one the wards come down and go back up, and she asked the crew for privacy while she worked, trade secrets, professional courtesy.

**Astrid** offered her an emergency chinchilla for sending messages. She took it for a snack first, asked whether it was true they taste like chocolate, and then let it climb onto her shoulder and named it Choco. She declined the parrot.

She spent the whole fight on the gondola clutching the rope with Choco in her ear, calming her and calling out where everyone was. At the last checkpoint **Ralo** and **Garran** finally watched her hands properly and found the invisible runes worked into the crate. She still has no idea anyone is studying her work.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 9

Still aboard at the brunch, still unpaid, and unsure how her guild's contracts settle. Her solution was to attach herself to **Astrid** on the theory that staying with the crew was how she would eventually get paid, which put her in the corridor outside the security vault at the worst possible moment.

She would not go to **Barran** herself. She had flirted with him on the gondola and had since come to her senses.

On the way down she studied the ship's construction, and knocked on the hull to explain it was cork, because all the magic in the world will not make a ship light. Astrid finally scared her off with **Pocket**. She is terrified of birds.

She kept Choco.
