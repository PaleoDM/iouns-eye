A Gutterwick woman, sister to **La Marginalia** ("Maggie"). Married to **Tomis Tidwell**.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 2

The crew found her drinking alone after dark, expecting her husband. She turned suspicious until **Lucien** charmed her through the cracked door and, helped by a wild magic surge that lit him up like an angel, convinced her they were there about her husband.

She talked. Tomis had always warned her to keep her distance from Maggie, then lately flipped, growing obsessed and pushing her to give her sister up — the rent collectors, he claimed, would forgive a year's rent in trade. Maggie had been wounded two weeks back and gone to ground in a safe house, and Wrenna would not betray her, no matter how often she and Tomis fought over it. She finally agreed to lead the crew to the safe house, also letting slip that Maggie meant to move contraband that very night. Then Tomis came home.
