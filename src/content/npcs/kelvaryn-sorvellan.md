Silver dragonborn patriarch of **House Sorvellan**, old money with the rot of generations of unchecked entitlement. He hired the crew at the **Brimstone Bath** to recover a set of compromising documents written by his son **Vendris** in youthful indiscretion.

Kelvaryn performs dignity in rooms he does not understand. He would not say much, but he gave up enough: the documents are politically a bad look and cannot be allowed to surface, with Vendris set to step into the family's council seat. The woman who took them goes by **La Marginalia**. His firm rule — the papers, bearing his own seal, must be returned before they reach the broadsheet printers.

He treats scoundrels with veiled disgust, and pays anyway. The job came in at 200 gold a head.
