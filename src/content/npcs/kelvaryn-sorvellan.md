Silver dragonborn patriarch of **House Sorvellan**, old money with the rot of generations of unchecked entitlement. He hired the crew at the **Brimstone Bath** to recover a set of compromising documents written by his son **Vendris** in youthful indiscretion.

Kelvaryn performs dignity in rooms he does not understand. He would not say much, but he gave up enough: the documents are politically a bad look and cannot be allowed to surface, with Vendris set to step into the family's council seat. The woman who took them goes by **La Marginalia**. His firm rule — the papers, bearing his own seal, must be returned before they reach the broadsheet printers.

He treats scoundrels with veiled disgust, and pays anyway. The job came in at 200 gold a head.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 3

Unseen, but his shadow lies over **Gutterwick**. **La Marginalia** holds him in open contempt for what his name does to the district, and the rent-men trailing the crew to the **Almshouse** answer, one way or another, to the house he heads. The crew also learned the job is only half-paid until they stop the manifesto at the press, which keeps Kelvaryn's purse very much in play.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 4

Still unseen, and a coward in his son's telling. Kelvaryn sent **Vendris** and a pack of hired goons to close out the job rather than appear himself, and sent them with orders to kill the crew once the deed changed hands. Vendris named him morally bankrupt and, more damning, likely bankrupt in coin too: the house could pay only half, which is the real reason it bleeds Gutterwick.
