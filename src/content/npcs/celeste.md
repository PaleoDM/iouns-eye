A gnomish woman who owns the bodega across the way from **Astrid**'s shop. An astrology junkie who has no idea Astrid's divination is the real thing — she thinks Astrid's whole act is a sad imitation of her own, and talks down to her accordingly.

Despite herself, Celeste passes along useful news: the **College of Divination** is bleeding members, enough that her own accepted place there has to wait, and one of the professors has gone missing. She's convinced that finding the professor will be her entrance exam.
