The late mother of **Vendris Sorvellan**. The **Inkwell** shophouse the crew now owns was hers for a long time, and she gifted the deed to Vendris, the one asset the boy could call his own before he signed it over to the crew.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 5

The locksmiths who changed the crew's locks knew the property as hers. They had once been hired to put a magical arcane lock on a secret door on the third floor, and never knew her password for it. With her gone, they were willing to hand over the manufacturer's reset password: Ironsmith. What the late Lady's relationship was to the **Silver Serpent**, whose den sat behind that door, is an open question.
