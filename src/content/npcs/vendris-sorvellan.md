The adult son and heir of **House Sorvellan**, silver dragonborn, recently of age and set to step into the family's hereditary council seat. As a younger man he wrote a set of documents — politically a bad look — that were taken by **La Marginalia**, a woman who had been close to him when she served as his private tutor.

Vendris attended the hire at the **Brimstone Bath** alongside his father **Kelvaryn**. Earnest and out of his depth among scoundrels. The documents that could destroy his appointment bear his own seal.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 3

His name surfaced again without him in the room. **La Marginalia** still carries a genuine soft spot for the younger Sorvellan, set against her contempt for his father. More dangerous: the manifesto in Vendris's own sealed hand is exactly what **Tabula Rasa** needs to calibrate the **Mimeofoil**. His youthful indiscretion is no longer just a political liability — it is the key to forging the city.
