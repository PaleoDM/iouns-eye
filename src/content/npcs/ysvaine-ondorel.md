Eldest daughter of **Lord Ondorel** and a working bard. She is the one who actually plays the **Evenbough**. She sails for **Farrowfield** to marry **Dolphus Garner**, and the marriage is her play rather than her cage.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 9

She was watching from the moment her father named **Barran** Hugentobler in front of the room.

She had been suspicious when **Ottavia Brann** brought him on, so she had him looked into, and Barran does not exist. No reputation, a resume that began and ended three weeks ago, and meanwhile half the houses in that room were asking her to vouch for him. She was not angry. She was curious, and it compelled her.

**Garran** lifted a bracelet off her wrist and let her see him do it, on the argument that the skill was resume enough. She told him to keep his secrets.
