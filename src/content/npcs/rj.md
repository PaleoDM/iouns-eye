Ralo Battaglia's young son. Lives with his mother, **Donna Bella "DB"**, who is currently in a trial separation from Ralo — a separation that is going badly. RJ is the emotional center of Ralo's life and the reason his elderly mother's apartment has become his safe house.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 7

**Stella Corvi** calls him Batty Junior and says he is getting tall. Ralo's own read is that he is the laziest person he has ever met and spends all his time with his shithead uncles. Stella told him to try talking to the boy sometime. Ralo says he tries all the time, and that it is all "we want stuff."
