A famous conductor of the **Glamour District** Symphony Orchestra.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 6

Quarterstaff never appeared in person. **Milo** took his identity twice over. First to run the con on **Sylvara**, selling her the story of a heartbroken first chair in need of a new lute, and then again at the gala itself, where the crew had the real Quarterstaff derailed before the evening so Milo could take the podium in his place.

That left Milo on the hook for actually conducting, which he did without a hitch, melding styles from different schools of conducting. Nobody in the room questioned him.

Two threads hang off this. Sylvara is a little bit smitten with Quarterstaff. And sooner or later it may get out that the real Quarterstaff was never there.
