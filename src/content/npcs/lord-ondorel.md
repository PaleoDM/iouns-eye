The head of **House Ondorel**, and the owner of the **Evenbough**. He does not take anyone's word about his own instrument. He authenticates it himself.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 8

He met the convoy at the **Witherwind** hangar at dawn on the 20th of Pelor, laid out several strips of ivory, worked a ritual, and pronounced the instrument real.

The ritual gave him the last memory carried on it, and he found the memory upsetting. He put it down to the poor first chair from the symphony being a bundle of nerves. He does not know **Sylvara** was the last person to hold it, at the portrait, with her nerves entirely about **Frederick Quarterstaff**.

He thanked the crew for bringing in **Renzo Corvi**, sent **Ottavia Brann** to place the instrument in the ship's vault herself, and then complicated everything by inviting them to the brunch before the sailing as his personal guests.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 9

He opened the wedding brunch aboard the *Meridian Queen* by naming **Barran** Hugentobler his special security detail in front of every noble aboard, calling it a strong old family name that half the room pretended to recognize. Since House Ondorel sails for a years-long tour after Farrowfield, he encouraged the room to hire the man while they still could.

Then a short wedding speech, and the introduction of his daughter's groom, **Dolphus Garner**.

He never learned that the **Evenbough** he authenticated with his own hands went out of the vault an hour later, or that what sails with him is **Sylvara**'s copy.
