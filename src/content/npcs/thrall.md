**Milo**'s long-suffering manservant, who insists on being called only *Thrall* — a holdover from the old vampire life neither he nor his master has quite let go of. Dry, patient, and used to suffering Milo's moods.

At the **Brimstone Bath** it was Thrall who went ahead to arrange for a patron to invite Milo in, since Milo will not enter a place uninvited. He asked **Astrid** to extend the invitation, and Milo made his entrance.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 8

**Milo** handed him over to **Astrid** as collateral for the loan of her pearl necklace. Thrall spent the morning copying out her field notes in illuminated calligraphy, capitals and marginal sketches and all, which left **Quill Bill** crestfallen at being passed over for the work.
