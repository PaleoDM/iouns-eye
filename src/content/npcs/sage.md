A former colleague of **Lucien**'s — elegant, beautiful, every hair in place, and unwilling to dirty her sleeves on the wrong kind of work. She brokers leads she does not care to run herself.

She handed Lucien the **Battaglia** matter: **Nonna Battaglia** had come to her seeking help with a legal challenge around her son's mutually agreed separation. Sage was loath to touch it because the Battaglia boy is tangled up with the **da Pinci** family. She was glad to pass it to someone who understood the real shape of the problem — the boy's brand and identity — and sent Lucien to the **Brimstone Bath** to meet him.
