A gnome gem cutter who has just made journeyman. She has curly blonde hair, a thick leather apron, and the tan lines of goggles around her eyes. Her father **Kendrin** named her for his favorite things, and her sister is Clarity.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 16

She was sweeping the step of her father's shop when the crew arrived, and she ripped a **Jelfi** campaign poster in half. She offered them resurrection diamonds, then told them her father had gone missing. Jelfi had gotten far too good, far too fast, and the guild heads were all wearing her rings. Facette had been whispering into the night for help, and as far as she was concerned, the crew was the answer.

Afterward she was back at her shop, still whispering into the night, and etching **Milo**'s likeness into an old gemstone. "You are the night. I whispered into the night, and you answered." A faint light came off her fingertips. Without meaning to, Milo has become her patron, and she has her very first cantrip.
