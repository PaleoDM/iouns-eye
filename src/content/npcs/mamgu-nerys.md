The village cook and hearth-keeper of Glancoed, and mother-hen to the whole place. Warm, flustered, and fierce about her kitchen and her chickens.

When the storm sealed the travelers in, it was Mamgu Nerys at the pot, cooking up a stew for everyone gathered under her roof. She flirted with Harsk, asking whether he might make her a wedding band out of the antlers of the buck he had hauled in, then put him to work rounding up the children for Indri's lesson. Her hearth is the warm heart of the village, and the one truly safe place in it.
