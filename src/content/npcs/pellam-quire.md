A halfling with dark curly hair, an ascot and tasteful jewelry, who wears his money like someone who has always had it. He speaks for a wealthy benefactor he will only call Mr. H.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 17

He came calling at the crew's shophouse in **Inkwell** with a job. The **Order of the Ju-Shi** is investigating Mr. H, and Mr. H would like the blame to land on **Severin Mortmain**, a senior Coinwright. The pay was five hundred gold apiece, a thousand more apiece for doing it well, or wondrous items from Mr. H's private enchanter. He brought a replica of Mortmain's signet ring for the swap.

**Astrid** offered to teach him bear. He said he found bears rather slothful, and she turned into one and chased him out of the house. He left his documents behind.
