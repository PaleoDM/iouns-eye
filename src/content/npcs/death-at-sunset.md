A great green dragon who once held the wood now called the Gloaming, and the reason that wood is dying.

The party learned of her through a memory the great alder gave to Indri. Five flying longboats, each with a spearhead at the prow and sailing fins down its flanks, ramming themselves into her hide. The first four shattered apart against her and rained down across the Gloaming, which is where its scattered giant-wrought wreckage comes from. The fifth went in true, burying itself between the collarbone and the back wing in the one place she was not armored. Dragon and longship came down together into a clearing a few hours north of the alder, through the untamed part of the wood where no path goes.

She has been dead for centuries. Her carcass is the source of *the sickening*: a green dragon's venom, in that quantity, left where it fell, seeping outward ever since. The Gloaming is still being killed by a dragon that died long before anyone in Glancoed was born.
