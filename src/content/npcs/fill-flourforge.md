Culdir's baker, and the town's great comeback story. He came to Culdir three or four years ago as a ruffian. An adventurer put him in his place, and now he bakes. He is the only one in town with a real bakery and a proper oven.

## Hearth and Hatchlight, Session 5

Fill entered a meat pie, flaky like a strudel, and got about five percent of the vote against Edith. He took it in stride. When Chippy called Edith a cheat, he clapped a hand over her mouth and told her it had never been about winning. One of the adventurers in the crowd was a very special man who had saved his life. Edith herself credits "that Laeryn" with setting him straight.

When Edith's shop caught fire, Fill was the first to shout for a bucket brigade. A half-eaten, scorched meat pie of his lay by her door afterward. Edith does not think he would stoop that low, but she has noticed his baking getting better, suspiciously so.

## Hearth and Hatchlight, Session 6

Fill is a dwarf, fifty or so. After the fire he took the sword down from over his mantel, put on his armor and went after **Briochebane** alone. The party found him at the tree line northeast of his farm, facing down a pair of bugbears. They had come for him on their master's orders, and they very nearly hauled him off. He recognized Rasa as Laeryn from a thrown javelin and told the rest of them everything.

As a young man he was a brigand. His crew tried to rob Laeryn's on the road, and Laeryn let him live and told him to turn it around. About five years ago he tried adventuring and took a job in the wood outside Culdir. Two miles in there is a house that does not belong there, warm and cozy, with a cellar full of the best food you can imagine. His whole party died there. In the larder he found a golden egg. He stayed in Culdir to keep watch on the wood, and last year Briochebane hatched.

Briochebane named himself, and he is the reason the meat pies are good. Fill calls him a friend, not a pet, and knows nothing about being a father. He swears he did not hurt Edith and does not know who set her fire. He agreed to answer for Briochebane's actions once he is home. He still has to tell Chippy.
