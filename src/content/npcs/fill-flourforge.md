Culdir's baker, and the town's great comeback story. He came to Culdir three or four years ago as a ruffian. An adventurer put him in his place, and now he bakes. He is the only one in town with a real bakery and a proper oven.

## Hearth and Hatchlight, Session 5

Fill entered a meat pie, flaky like a strudel, and got about five percent of the vote against Edith. He took it in stride. When Chippy called Edith a cheat, he clapped a hand over her mouth and told her it had never been about winning. One of the adventurers in the crowd was a very special man who had saved his life. Edith herself credits "that Laeryn" with setting him straight.

When Edith's shop caught fire, Fill was the first to shout for a bucket brigade. A half-eaten, scorched meat pie of his lay by her door afterward. Edith does not think he would stoop that low, but she has noticed his baking getting better, suspiciously so.
