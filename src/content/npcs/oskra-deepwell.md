The dwarven foreman of the licensed **Widow Crane Rookery** on the cliffs above the **Inkwell**. Her harvesters know the ritual dance that lets them close to a mourning hen, and by old custom they never take more than a third of her tears. Nobody else can do the work, so she sells to everyone.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 12

Turned **Olivier** away from the cliffs and called him a narc when he came back with the crew. **Astrid** talked her way in on a fake welfare inspection, and once Olivier's crystal was out of the room Oskra talked. The colony was shrinking, and some nests had been emptied with no sign of a fight. Pushed harder, she admitted that a month ago she took a sack of coin to let a young son of the **Irisour Family** take a lady he was courting through the nests at night. When Astrid told her she had made the stars very angry, she made **Sehanine**'s sign before she could stop herself.

She escorted the crew down the cliff, and two of her harvesters, **Pim Rattlecord** and **Sefa Ondu**, came back full of manticore spikes. When Astrid told her poachers were stealing chicks, Oskra called her the baddest person she knew after her mom and grandma, cast Shield of Faith on her, and took her wounded back up to untangle her ledger from a noble boy.
