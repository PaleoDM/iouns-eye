The elemental titan of storm and wind, by the name Harsk's people give him. In Orcish the name means something like the one who goes at the vanguard, because he always charges first into battle.

At Culdir he set aside his squabbles with Morosko, just this once, to stop a giant advance through the neck of the peninsula. The giants' army still stands frozen in the north field.
