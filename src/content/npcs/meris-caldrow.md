Mother of four who hired the party to rescue her family. Her infant daughter Ellie is an Aasimar, making the family a target of the Celestial Seed cult. Apprenticed under Rooker in magical studies and, in the campaign's epilogue, became one of the key archivists of the arcane school he helped found alongside Headmistress Sombra.
