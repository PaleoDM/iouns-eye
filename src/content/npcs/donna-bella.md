**Ralo Battaglia**'s separated wife and **RJ**'s mother. Goes by DB.

The separation has been going badly for a while. By Ralo's account she walked out because he would not buy a boat. By everyone else's account there is more to it than that.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 7

Named on screen for the first time. **Stella Corvi** knows her, likes her, and thinks Ralo is the one who blew it. Stella had drinks with her the week before and reported that DB has stopped sending assassins after Ralo, which Stella considers a worrying sign rather than a good one.

Stella told Ralo she would pass along that he was still thinking about her.
