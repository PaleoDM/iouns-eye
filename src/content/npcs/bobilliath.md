A dwarf with an orange beard and hair, "Bobbi" for short. A mutual acquaintance and working contact of **Garran**. The two drink together and trade leads. It was Bobbi who heard a job was going down at the **Brimstone Bath** — though he couldn't say whether the job was happening *there* or whether that was just where you went to hear about it — and brought Garran in on the buddy system.
