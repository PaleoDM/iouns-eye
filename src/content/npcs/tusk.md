**Severin Mortmain**'s special pet, a warthog the size of an elephant that stands twelve or fifteen feet at the shoulder. Part of it is machine. It has a steel plate on one shoulder, part of its jaw in steel, an antenna off the back of its head, and a light that keeps scanning even in its sleep. Its steel chest plate has a hinged door in it, like a small safe. Mortmain is said to give it his signet ring at night.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 17

**Astrid** saw it first through a toucan's eyes, asleep in the hay. When the stables caught fire, **Bertie Gristle** led it out and sent it to the manor, where it sleeps on a huge rug in front of the fireplace. Astrid cast animal friendship on it, and it took. Then something else in its head pushed back at her. There were two things in that head, and she shut the second one out.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 18

**Astrid** drugged it with the draught in the chimney, and it slept with its eyes open on its left side, right on top of the hatch. When **Lucien** tried a spell on it, the second thing in its head stabbed into his mind. Astrid's Animal Friendship held, and at her word the hog rolled over. **Ralo** popped the hinge pin and swapped the signet ring for the fake. The thing in its head lashed out with the sleeping body. Astrid's Guiding Bolt lit up an invisible leech latched on the back of Tusk's head, like a lamprey on a shark. It let go and went for **Garran**, and **Stardust** crushed it. Tusk was no worse for wear.
