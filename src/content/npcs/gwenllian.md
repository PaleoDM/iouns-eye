The headwoman of Glancoed. Capable, respected, and in her prime, not an elder. Pragmatic and quietly frightened.

Over Mamgu Nerys's stew, she made the ask the village had no one else to make: she asked the storm-stranded travelers to go into the Gloaming and find Eirlys, the village witch, gone three days into the wood. In return she offered them a permanent roof in Glancoed, warm beds, hot meals, and whatever treasure they could carry out. She knew she was sending strangers into the dark because there was no one else to send.
