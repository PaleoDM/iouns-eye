The man who watches contraband at **The GoldSoul**, the drophouse buried under a temple of Glittergold. Takes a customary five gold to hold goods, no questions asked. **Garran** knows him.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 4

Held the crew's stolen broadsheets and the manifesto's linocut plate. Beyond his usual fee, he asked Garran to put in a good word with **Ralo** — now that Ralo had split from the missus, Zedediah fancied sharing a pint and a bite with him sometime. Garran agreed to pass it along.
