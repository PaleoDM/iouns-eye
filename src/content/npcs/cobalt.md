Tiefling proprietor of the **Brimstone Bath**, a working-class bath house she runs as neutral ground in the city's turf wars. Pragmatic and unflappable, she keeps a tight room and a ready set of bouncers.

When the Sorvellans turned up at her bath without paying for use of a private meeting room, Cobalt assumed she was being hit — she recognized **Ralo Battaglia** and figured a job was being pulled on her. The misunderstanding was talked down, a couple of coins (Astrid's, as it happened) changed hands, and the party got their private room. Cobalt remembers faces and debts.
