A master gem cutter of **Gemheart** and **Facette**'s father. His shop has stood for generations. He has won six consecutive elections to the guild council, and according to his daughter, everyone loves him.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 16

He went missing during Glittergold's Holy Week, with the guild election closing that night. A **Jelfi** poster outside his shop said he would abandon you like he had abandoned the race. The crew found him alive in the cellar of Jelfi's shop. On election night she had meant to crystallize his heart and eat it, and keep his skill forever. He owes the crew some powerful favors.
