The sensei who ran out of patience with Bringha's improvisations and pushed her out the dojo door with a parcel to deliver to a village called Glancoed. Taped to it was a note: "just because the mighty elm can sway in the breeze does not mean it does not also need to put down firm roots."

She went unnamed until Session 4, when Bringha finally delivered the parcel. Inside was ankheg milk, poison at full strength and a powerful tranquilizer diluted, which Eirlys had requested from her directly. A message came with the bottle, and Eirlys kept it for herself.

How the sensei of a distant dojo comes to be filling orders for the witch of Glancoed has not been explained.
