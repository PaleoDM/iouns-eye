A rival scoundrel working Rifthaven, and the man who killed **Lonnie "Lobster" da Pinci**.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 6

Corvi turned up at the gala planted in the cocktail band, running his own con on the same instrument the crew came for. **Ralo** clocked him and recognized him immediately.

Ralo also supplied the piece nobody in the syndicate had. Lonnie had been sleeping with Corvi's wife. What everyone had read as a crime between two families was in fact a crime of passion, and it has complicated the relationship between those families ever since.

Ralo passed the sighting to **Astrid**, who got it to **Garran**, and that chain is what kept the night intact. When Corvi moved for the instrument, Garran was already in position. Because of the crowd he did not go to violence. He used his fear aura to back Corvi down, kept him from getting closer, and eventually got him around a corner and fully intimidated into leaving.

Corvi walked out alive, knowing someone else is working the same score.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 7

**Ottavia Brann** put a bounty on him. She knows someone tried to steal the **Evenbough** and that someone impersonated **Frederick Quarterstaff**, and she does not know those were two different crews. She paid **Garran** 250 gold to bring Corvi in, alive if possible, because **House Ondorel** wants to question him.

His wife **Stella** gave him up. She had already made peace with the fact that he was going to die, and she had tried to talk him out of the lute job in the first place, because getting back into the **Gatitos**' good graces by stealing an instrument was never going to work.

The crew found him at the **Lamplighter Market** with the **Corvi Five**, arguing about whether to bail on the job. What followed was the second consecutive night he was made a fool of. **Milo** hit him with Hold Person; he rolled a natural 20 on the save, and lost it anyway through two forced rerolls. Later Milo hit him with Tasha's Hideous Laughter, and then hit him with it again. He spent most of the fight paralyzed or laughing on the ground while four of his crew died around him.

The crew had him surrounded and every right to finish it. They took him alive instead, for the bonus and for the goodwill.

**He is in House Ondorel's hands, and he can tell them he never wore Quarterstaff's face.**
