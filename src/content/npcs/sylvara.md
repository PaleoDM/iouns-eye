A luthier the crew does not know, but who appears to be a neighbor in **Inkwell**. When the crew worked out that the Songbird job needs a magical decoy, she was the only craftsman they could name who might build one.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 6

The crew decided Sylvara was a civilian, and that she needed to be conned rather than cut in. **Milo** and **Lucien** approached her in disguise, as the famous conductor **Frederick Quarterstaff** and his first chair **Alon Bardoof**. Quarterstaff sold her on the idea that Bardoof is an objectum sexual who had recently broken up with his lute and would never play again unless somebody found him an instrument of equal majesty and beauty. She bought all of it. She came away very smitten with Quarterstaff and very dedicated to finding Bardoof his new lute, and she volunteered that the instrument would be on display that night at a fancy gala.

The crew bought her a very expensive ticket that put her close to the action, with **Ralo** playing Quarterstaff's driver to ferry her around. Her seat alone accounted for three or four of the eight pieces of information she needs to build the duplicate, and Quarterstaff's fuss over a portrait needing "feminine hands" put the instrument physically in her grip in front of the room.

**The eight she needs:** the type of wood, the sound it makes, the maker's mark, the school of magic, the wear pattern, any inscription, the feeling it gives when you touch it, and the case itself. The crew collected all eight by the end of the night.

She still believes she is helping a lovesick musician, and she still believes Quarterstaff is who he says he is.
