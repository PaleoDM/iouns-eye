Fill Flourforge's apprentice, a girl of maybe twelve. Fiercely loyal to her master. Fill says that someday she will inherit the shop.

## Hearth and Hatchlight, Session 5

Chippy tried to buy Slate off Indri at the festival. She called him the second cool little lizard with wings that totally isn't a dragon thing she had seen that day. She decided Indri was probably a spy, and Indri made an arch-nemesis in about five minutes. Bringha tied Chippy's shoelaces together. When Chippy got up to comfort Fill after the judging, she went face first into a pie.

She told Fill that Edith was cheating and did not even have an oven, and she stormed off. According to Peetra Sylver, Chippy came to Edith's shop before the fire, found the door locked, and stormed off back toward Fill's.
