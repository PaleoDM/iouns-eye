First chair of the **Glamour District** Symphony Orchestra, under **Frederick Quarterstaff**.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 6

Bardoof never appeared in person either. **Lucien** wore his identity to run the con on **Sylvara**, where Quarterstaff explained that Bardoof is an objectum sexual, someone romantically attracted to and involved with objects, in his case lutes. As the story went, Bardoof had recently broken up with his previous lute and was likely never to play again unless he could find an instrument of equal majesty and beauty.

Bardoof was not playing the gala, which gave Lucien the freedom to arrive as himself and take a different seat entirely.
