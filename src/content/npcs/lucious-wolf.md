A divorce attorney who advertises in the drunk tank. The pitch is simple. The boys spend a night in the tank, their ladies refuse to post bail, and the boys get ideas. The flyer shows a heavy man with greasy slicked-back hair and his boots up.

Somewhere in the city, a doppelganger has been using **Lucien**'s likeness to do it. That explains the ad **Ralo** saw in the urinal at Bennigan's in Session 1.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 16

A halfling bricklayer at the **Gemheart** Guild Hall took Lucien for Mr. Wolf and was thrilled to see him down there in person. He still had the flyer. He decided it must be an old illusion spell.
