A mid-tier boss in the **da Pinci Crime Syndicate**, and **Ralo Battaglia**'s former boss. Killed shortly before the events of *Knaves and Knights*. His death destabilized his stretch of the operation and put the pressure on Ralo to step up and fill the gap, pressure Ralo does not want.

The syndicate's read on the killing was that Lonnie had been quietly paying the **Aletheia Procedure** for protection, and when those payments lapsed his rivals saw the opening and moved on him.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 6

That read was wrong, or at least incomplete. **Renzo "Basta" Corvi** killed him, and **Ralo** told the table why. Lonnie had been sleeping with Corvi's wife. What everyone had taken for a crime between two families was in fact a crime of passion, and it has complicated the relationship between those families ever since.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 7

That read was wrong too.

**Stella Corvi** told **Ralo** herself. She was never sleeping with Lonnie. Not yet. She was working him. She is **Gatito** by blood, and the Gatitos and the da Pincis were close to a truce with Lonnie as the way in. By her account he was a nice man, a complete and total fool, and wrapped around her finger without ever knowing it.

Renzo killed him out of jealousy over a woman who was working, and killed the truce along with the man. The two families have held an uneasy peace since, and nobody on either side knows the real shape of what was lost.
