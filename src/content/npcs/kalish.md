**Olivier**'s crystal man, the one who records the footage for his late-night nature show on the Aether-waves.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 12

Went onto the crane cliffs at night with Olivier and recorded something huge and gangly crossing the dark behind the nests. Then came a scream. Olivier ran and could not make himself stop, and Kalish slipped behind him. He has not been seen since. The crystal he was recording on came back cracked.
