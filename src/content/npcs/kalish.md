**Olivier**'s crystal man, the one who records the footage for his late-night nature show on the Aether-waves.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 12

Went onto the crane cliffs at night with Olivier and recorded something huge and gangly crossing the dark behind the nests. Then came a scream. Olivier ran and could not make himself stop, and Kalish slipped behind him. He has not been seen since. The crystal he was recording on came back cracked.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 13

Alive. The crew found him in a cage in the **Circle of the Broken Tusk**'s den, squinting away from the light. **Olivier** skirted the chained **Livid** to get to him, which technically finished the job.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 14

With some food and water in him, he told the crew what he saw while caged. The **Circle of the Broken Tusk**'s camp is built across ledges a couple of quarries over, and its matron holds court in a cavern on one of the upper layers. He heard **Astrid** explain the single punishment for poachers and took it for an on-screen bit. He called it ratings gold and asked for a named rival, and Astrid gave him the Broken Tusk. He rode into the camp in a chest with a pillow, alongside **Olivier**.
