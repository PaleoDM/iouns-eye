A gold dragon wyrmling about the size of a house cat, and the reason Fill Flourforge's meat pies are so good. He hatched last year from a golden egg Fill found in a larder in the wood outside Culdir. He named himself. Fill calls him a friend, not a pet, and admits he knows nothing about being a father.

He lived in a hidden alcove built into the back of Fill's good oven and fired it from inside. Chippy saw him at the festival and called him a cool little lizard with wings that totally isn't a dragon thing.

## Hearth and Hatchlight, Sessions 5 and 6

Peetra Sylver saw a yellow lizard, which he called the size of a goat, come up out of Edith's chimney during the fire and fly off northeast. In Fill's oven the party found a freshly shed gold scale and a cone of scorch marks, and more scales led toward the trees. Something in the wood lured him in. Its bugbears say their master wants the wyrm, and that they will take good care of him. He would live forever.

He is small enough that he could not have barricaded Edith's door. Whether he started the fire, nobody can say yet.
