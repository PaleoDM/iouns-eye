A tiefling farrier who shapes iron with her bare hands. The head of the Farriers Guild retired this year, and she is the favorite for the seat, though not a sure thing. She wears no jewelry, not even in her horns. Her platform is to throw the bankers out of the guild, which is not popular.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 16

She told **Lucien** he was awfully pretty, and that she had taken a hundred meetings with his type this week. His Detect Thoughts caught her thinking that his ascot was too nice for the district, and that he had used five hedge words in a ten-word sentence. Plenty of her colleagues had suddenly taken to jewelry, she said, but her hands were always in the fire. She had been gaining ground until she lost a supporter. She was not surprised **Olo** had been bought, only by how many others had.
