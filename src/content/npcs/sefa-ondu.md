One of the dance masters at the **Widow Crane Rookery**, under **Oskra Deepwell**.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 12

Winched the crew down the cliff with **Pim Rattlecord**, and took manticore spikes fighting one off up ahead of the party. **Garran** helped carry the wounded to the first nesting plateau, where the two of them coached **Milo** through the crane dance from the ground.
