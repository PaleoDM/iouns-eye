Farrowfield grain money, sailing to marry **Ysvaine Ondorel**. The face of a soft nerd and the hands of a farmer, and genuinely pleased about the wedding.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 9

An assassin of the **Third Act** came for him at his own wedding brunch with a silvered pistol. **Milo** was standing in front of him when she fired, so nobody in the room could say which of them she meant. Milo then put Mirror Image on him, and both her remaining shots took a duplicate.

He lent Milo his sword for the toast: a nigh untarnishable blade commissioned from the Farrowfield artificer **Arlowe**, to celebrate the agricultural renaissance following the siege.

What the Third Act believes him to be is an open question.
