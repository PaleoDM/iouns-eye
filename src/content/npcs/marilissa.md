The larger of the two snakes that poisoned the great alder and took Eirlys, and the one able to wear a human shape. She kept Eirlys manacled in a chamber inside Death at Sunset's lair and put her to work at a bench there.

Marilissa did not strike a bargain. She was *called*, and she believed it completely. Sunset-Is-Nigh reached for her and she came, and by the time the party found her she had been taking people from further afield than Glancoed for a good while without anyone coming looking. Rhydderch and the other missing were hers: not raised so much as posted, each one placed where she wanted them standing, guarding the breach into the lair.

She had been witch-cursed. She crossed a witch somewhere behind all of this, and the curse took her spellcasting from her, which is why the party met a woman with two venomous snakes and her own hands rather than a spellcaster. Who cursed her, and what she did to earn it, is not known.

The party found her out of patience. Five days of Eirlys's slow work and wrong answers had gotten her nothing, and she was beating her prisoner when they arrived. Yu had already killed her snake spy a corridor away, so she never got the warning she was owed. The fight went badly for the party in the first round, with venom doing real damage and Harsk going down, then turned hard when Marilissa began missing. Laeryn landed the killing blow.
