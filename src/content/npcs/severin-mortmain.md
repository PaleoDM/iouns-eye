An old elf with thinning hair, and a senior Coinwright. He wears his signet ring every waking hour, with a falcon on the seal, and he is warded against charm. At night he gives the ring to his special pet, **Tusk**.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 17

The mark of the One Ring Job. The **Order of the Ju-Shi** may decide that souls have been trafficked between financial institutions and extraplanar entities, and Mr. H hired the crew through **Pellam Quire** to make sure it decides that about Mortmain. He is under a loose house arrest, and he sent his family abroad to keep them clear of the investigation.

He was bored enough to host an afternoon party, and the crew got in as wine merchants. He turned out to be an agreeable fellow who chats with the help. **Ralo** handed him a bell pepper carved into a tulip, and he said Ralo had fast hands. He told his neighbor **Dahlia** that he has never claimed to be a saint, but he had no idea what the investigation was about.

His study holds a book that is really a box, alarmed so that only someone carrying the ring can lift it. **Lucien** left it alone, and took a quicksilver potion out of the locked desk drawer instead.
