Tabaxi librarian and administrative assistant at Ishetar's mage academy. Works closely with Rooker despite their contentious relationship — introduced arguing with him over an overdue library book. Takes first billing on their co-authored research notes.

In the campaign's epilogue (Session 34), Sombra was installed as Headmistress of the arcane school Rooker helped found, with Meris Caldrow as one of its key archivists. A second campus was planned in the Red Vale to take in Vegepygmy children with the gift.
