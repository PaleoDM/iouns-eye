An old-money elf who heads the Basket Weavers Guild. Her guild is one of the most tightly woven communities in **Gemheart**, and it votes as a block, which has swung many an election.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 16

**Lucien** noticed the dent of a ring on her finger, and no ring. **Astrid**'s Detect Magic found the ring there anyway, invisible and ringing with enchantment and illusion. It turned visible when **Milo** dispelled **Jelfi**.
