**Severin Mortmain**'s stablemaster, a big half-orc in farrier's leathers, with goggles and an arcane screwdriver. He feeds the house toucans scrap fruit and looks after **Tusk**.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 17

**Astrid** came to the estate as Lars, the stable boy driving the caterers' cart. Bertie watched her work until he was satisfied she knew horses. When **Garran**'s toucan set the stables on fire, he threw open the doors and led Tusk out, and told it that it was sleeping in the manor tonight anyway.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 18

**Astrid** watched him lock every door and window of the manor, and set his alarm on the front door only. Later that night he stood with **Severin Mortmain** at the burned stables with a hooded lantern. He agreed the fire was suspicious, but nothing there had to do with the investigation. In the morning he was to make the fire look worse and bring down a beam or two over their entrance. He asked about the usual wards against the fey, and Mortmain switched them to fiends.
