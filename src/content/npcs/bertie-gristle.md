**Severin Mortmain**'s stablemaster, a big half-orc in farrier's leathers, with goggles and an arcane screwdriver. He feeds the house toucans scrap fruit and looks after **Tusk**.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 17

**Astrid** came to the estate as Lars, the stable boy driving the caterers' cart. Bertie watched her work until he was satisfied she knew horses. When **Garran**'s toucan set the stables on fire, he threw open the doors and led Tusk out, and told it that it was sleeping in the manor tonight anyway.
