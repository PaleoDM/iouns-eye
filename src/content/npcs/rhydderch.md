A trapper who worked the Gloaming alone. Known to Glancoed, and known at least in passing to Harsk: the two of them overlapped on the road at some point and shared a fire at Rhydderch's camp, which is the only reason Harsk could find the paths through that wood at all.

He stopped coming out of the Gloaming. His disappearance, as much as the sickening itself, is what sent Eirlys into the wood. When the party reached his camp they found it interrupted rather than abandoned, the fire recent, his whole life sitting there mid-chore with nobody in it. Eirlys had been there before them, and her bootprints led on from the camp upstream to the great alder.

Rhydderch was found in Session 3, at the breach into Death at Sunset's lair. He was dead, and he had been put to work. Marilissa had not simply raised him. She had posted him, standing rather than shambling, with his own snares strung up around him and the missing shepherds and drovers standing to either side. Somebody had chosen where each of them would stand.

He walked through a flask of alchemist's fire without slowing and burned for the whole of the fight. Harsk, who had shared a fire at his camp once, put the arrow through him that put him down, and gave him a farewell afterward. Quiet, and short, and the only one he was ever going to get.
