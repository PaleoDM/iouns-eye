The beloved village witch of Glancoed, a gentle herbwife of kindness and poultices. Also the keeper of the village's small rites: she left the offerings at the spring-shrine and sang the morning water-song.

Eirlys walked into the Gloaming three days before the travelers arrived, hunting the source of *the sickening* spreading through the wood, and she has not come back. Her absence is felt everywhere. The spring's afanc went cross and frightened when her offerings stopped, and the headwoman Gwenllian asked the strangers to brave the forest and find her. She did not appear this session, present only through the hole she left behind.

Her notes, brought out of her hut by Gwenllian, made her reasoning plain: the sickening was old news, but people had begun going missing and the great alder deep in the wood was getting sicker. She went in for both, to heal the tree and to find Rhydderch the trapper. The party followed her trail as far as the alder, where it stopped being a trail at all. She was lured there deliberately. A pair of snakes, one small and one large and one of them able to wear a human shape, poisoned the tree on purpose to draw her out, then took her and dragged her away. There is a splash of acid burned into the back of the trunk where she tried to fight them off.

She was taken, not found dead. Where she was dragged to, and in what condition she arrived, is not yet known.
