The beloved village witch of Glancoed, a gentle herbwife of kindness and poultices. Also the keeper of the village's small rites: she left the offerings at the spring-shrine and sang the morning water-song.

Eirlys walked into the Gloaming three days before the travelers arrived, hunting the source of *the sickening* spreading through the wood, and she has not come back. Her absence is felt everywhere. The spring's afanc went cross and frightened when her offerings stopped, and the headwoman Gwenllian asked the strangers to brave the forest and find her. She did not appear this session, present only through the hole she left behind.
