One of the dance masters at the **Widow Crane Rookery**, under **Oskra Deepwell**. Spent eight years at bard school learning the craft.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 12

Winched the crew down the cliff with **Sefa Ondu**, and took manticore spikes fighting one off up ahead of the party. In no shape to dance, Pim coached **Milo** through the crane dance from the ground instead. Milo rolled a 37 on his first try, and Pim took it badly.
