## Background

Emissary Rowena is the colony leader of Ishetar who tasks the party with mapping the surrounding region and establishing diplomatic relations with local peoples. She serves as a primary quest-giver for the campaign.

## Epilogue

After Sallow's defeat (Session 34), Rowena set Lyra to a long task: bringing the people of Ekkorai together under one banner. The work spanned Firewatch Island, the Vegepygmy communities, the logging camps, and every quiet community along the way.
