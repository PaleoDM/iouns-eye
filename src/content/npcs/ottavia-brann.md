Head of security at the gala where the **Ondorel** instrument was displayed. Hard to impress and harder to fool.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 6

Brann was not impressed by the business with the portrait, and only backed off after **Milo**, wearing **Frederick Quarterstaff**'s face, talked her down.

What actually won her over was **Garran**. Working the room as backstage security, he posed as house security sweeping for bugs and cast Detect Magic on the instrument, which got the crew the last of the eight pieces they needed. It extremely impressed Brann, who offered him a spot on the extra hired security in two weeks' time, when the instrument gets loaded onto the *Meridian Queen*.

**Important:** Brann does not know Garran as Garran. She knows him as **Barran**.
