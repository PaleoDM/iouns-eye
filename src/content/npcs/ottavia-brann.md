Head of security at the gala where the **Ondorel** instrument was displayed. Hard to impress and harder to fool.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 6

Brann was not impressed by the business with the portrait, and only backed off after **Milo**, wearing **Frederick Quarterstaff**'s face, talked her down.

What actually won her over was **Garran**. Working the room as backstage security, he posed as house security sweeping for bugs and cast Detect Magic on the instrument, which got the crew the last of the eight pieces they needed. It extremely impressed Brann, who offered him a spot on the extra hired security in two weeks' time, when the instrument gets loaded onto the *Meridian Queen*.

**Important:** Brann does not know Garran as Garran. She knows him as **Barran**.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 7

Two days after the gala she came to Barran with work. She knows two things about that night. Someone tried to steal the **Evenbough**, and someone impersonated **Frederick Quarterstaff**. She does not know those were two different crews, which means she is looking for the crew that hired her.

She put 250 gold in his hand up front and told him to assemble a team, not knowing he already had one and was standing in a room with all of them. The job was **Renzo Corvi**, alive if it could be managed, because **House Ondorel** wants to question him.

She offered what leads she had: the real Quarterstaff, whoever booked the cocktail band, the kitchen and event staff.

The crew brought Corvi in breathing. She owes them fifty gold more for it, and she is the woman running security on the thing they actually came to steal.
