The Widowmaker of the old Goliath song, and once a widow crane. She was not allowed to mourn, and it changed her the way a werewolf is changed. She is about the size of a young dragon, with slate-gray hide, obsidian plates down her back, and the long beak and crest of a pterodactyl. Her glands have burst into garnet crystals. The other cranes do not call her kin. They call her a monster.

She says she had a real name once. Livid is the one she goes by now.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 13

The crew found her chained, muzzled and bled dry in a den run by the **Circle of the Broken Tusk**, her glands cut open and drained for **Widow's Bane**. **Milo** danced the crane dance until her rage dimmed, and **Astrid** spoke with her and cast **The Widow** across the cavern ceiling. Livid asked for her chicks. She described her hatred as a chain around her heart, and said that when it takes her she would kill anything at all. Milo gave her a sad couplet about a bottle, and puzzling over it kept the rage off her.

Astrid unshackled her. Livid gave the crew directions to the poachers' nest, three quarries over and down a hidden ravine, and the last two drops of Bane from her glands. She has been told that griffins are friends, not food. That was a hard one for her.

When five poachers came back on their skiff, **Lucien** called her out with the crane's call while they stood frozen in Milo's Hypnotic Pattern. She ate all five, including the one on fire, then looked northwest toward her chicks and went back into the cave to rest.
