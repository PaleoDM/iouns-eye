A drunk who sits drinking under a tree near Edith's shop. It is easy to look straight past him, and he sees more than people think.

## Hearth and Hatchlight, Session 5

Yu noticed him after the fire. He had seen the whole thing, and it had taken them long enough. He liked Yu's hat. He wanted a bottle of whiskey for the rest, and he settled for five silver. According to him, Chippy came first, found the door locked, and stormed off toward Fill's. Then a big yellow lizard about the size of a goat lit the place up and flew off northeast toward the woods. He knows lizards do not fly. He started to introduce himself and passed out halfway through.

## Hearth and Hatchlight, Session 6

Still passed out under his tree while the party took a short rest beside him. Yu wanted to cast spare the dying on him. He was not dying, just drunk, and not even hungover yet. His goat-sized lizard turned out to be about the size of a house cat.
