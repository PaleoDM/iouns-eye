A gnome who runs the **Halworth Reading Rooms**, kept company by a cat. Genteel and fussy, oblivious to the society of dead scholars in the attic above her shop.

## Knaves and Knights, Session 2

Met the crew at the door. When they pressed her about others who had come asking after **La Marginalia**, she could not hold a description still — a man, a woman, elvish, dwarven, the answer kept changing. Then her nose began to bleed, except **Milo** saw it was not blood but ink. Her memory of the earlier visitors had been written over, the ink leaking from the hole left behind. The crew read it as the fingerprint of whoever is scraping records ahead of them.
