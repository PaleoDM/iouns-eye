Vicas's deceased wife. In her own right, a fantastic adventurer who traveled widely while Vicas stayed home in their small fishing community tending mundane injuries. She fell to a wasting sickness no one could cure. When she took a final turn for the worse, Vicas brewed her a cup of tea with his own toxins and let her rest. Kato found out, accused Vicas of denying her the Dawnfather's plan, and ran him out of town. That is how Vicas came to Ishetar.

A banshee later confirmed she had escorted Shallah personally, and showed Vicas a vision: Shallah seated at a great hall among the fiercest warriors of the dead, toasted for her glorious end, welcomed and worthy. In his own epilogue, years after the fall of Sallow, Vicas walked the trails Shallah had walked, retracing her old stories and getting to know her in the way he hadn't been able to in life — before returning to Ishetar, beneath the maple tree at the grave Thump had prepared, to join her.
